A SECOND, INDEPENDENT WHO SERIES CONFIRMS THE 1978-1986 GAP
  The WER weekly "Infected Areas" lists, published under the International Health Regulations,
  name every area declared infected with cholera, plague or yellow fever. Twelve issues were
  pulled from IRIS and inspected:
    24 Sep 1970, 12 Nov 1970  -> Angola absent entirely
    26 Oct 1978, 19 Jul 1979, 2 Oct 1980, 17 Dec 1981, 12 Aug 1982, 2 Dec 1982, 27 Jan 1983
                              -> Angola listed under YELLOW FEVER only, never under CHOLERA
    1 Nov 1984, 26 Sep 1985, 6 Nov 1986 -> Angola absent entirely
    4 Jun 1987, 2 Jul 1987    -> Angola listed under CHOLERA
  Angola was therefore actively making IHR declarations throughout the gap (it was declaring
  yellow-fever infected areas), which is what separates this from a silent surveillance system.
  Corroboration note appended to all nine 1978-1986 rows and to the 1970 row. The 1982 row was
  UPGRADED from Inferred_Absence 0.7 to Documented_Absence 0.8 on the strength of the 12 August
  and 2 December 1982 lists.
  Counter-weight recorded honestly: a 2007 assessment quoted in the press states Angola had "no
  epidemiological surveillance system" during and after the war, which is why these rows sit at
  0.8 rather than at the Level-1 floor of 0.9-1.0 and are marked surveillance=partial.

  The 3287-day 1978-1986 gap was closed not by finding case counts - there are none -
