3. GAPS STILL OPEN - AND WHETHER THE DATA EXIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 1976: surveillance gap. WHO never received a figure (Angola bound by the IHR only from 15 Aug 1976); press in four
  languages, the WER and JPRS are silent. A national count almost certainly does not exist; leads are print-only
  (Henriques 1976; JPRS Epidemiology Nos. 66-75).
* Feb 1997-Nov 1998: present, count unknown (last WHO notification ends 7 Feb 1997; MSF reports unconfirmed Luanda
  cases). A count could exist only in MSF/MINSA paper archives.
* Dec 1998-Apr 2002: surveillance gap (war); WHO itself blames lack of surveillance. No count is likely to exist.
* May 2002-Dec 2003: surveillance gap for cholera. Circumstantially quiet (dense humanitarian presence; shigellosis,
  not cholera, in enteric outbreaks; a new strain in 2006), but AFRO marks cholera 'not reported', so no zero.
* Jun-Dec 2004: surveillance gap (IDSR weekly reports stop after week 22). 2005: the JHU/WHO zero is plausible.
* From 2026-09-20: reporting lag. MoH week 37 (116, Index 366) is inferred (0.5). As of 30 Sep, no source has
  published weeks 38-39 (Africa CDC, WHO dashboard, ECDC, press checked).
* Thin granularity: national sub-annual counts for Jan-Oct 2008, H2 2010, 2011 and 2015-16; provincial 2010-15; the
  unresolved 2015 'Colera 50' (Index 108 stays an inferred zero at 0.4).

5. WHY MOST AI ROWS DO NOT REACH THE MODEL (builder/JHU defects - reported; not fixable in the AI layer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* JHU totals are spread at full value over the weeks JHU's own weekly rows leave empty, never netted against them:
  2006 = 118 764 in the model (WHO 67 257), 2007 = 75 052 (WHO 18 422); 14 560 cases land in 2006 weeks 1-7, before
  the 13 Feb onset. Bad JHU rows feed this: 555 (wrong dates), 307/517 (2007 annual repeated), 583 (digit error).
* A JHU date typo (ObservationCollection5014 lines 73-74 and 78-79: 2023-02-04 for 2018-02-04, reporting week
  'W07 2018') injects 61-177 phantom cases a year into 2019-22, overriding the WHO 2021 zero and the yearbook zeros.
* JHU 1988 (15 500) is below WHO's own Jan-Jul notifications (16 351). The JHU seasonal template misplaces the 1972
  peak (Apr-May vs a notified January) and puts 205 cases in late 1974 (22 notified). JHU Bie 2006 (4 080) is a
  mislabel (Bie reported 22-46).
* Rows of 7 days or fewer count as whole weeks. All 2026 weeks (WHO and AI alike) are keyed 6 days early, but
  consistently, so nothing is lost. JHU outranks AI, hiding complete WER series for 1971-77, 1987-97 and 2006-07.
* Surveillance-gap weeks reach the model as assumed_zero (0 cases, 0.5) unless the MOSAIC import treats them as
  missing.
* Whole cycle vs the pre-cycle file (same baselines): 1 051 of 2 959 weeks changed. 1993: 0 -> 11 210; 2026:
  9 977 -> 6 730; 1997: 9 -> 10; zero relabels in 1976, 1978-86 (and ISO week 1 of 1987), 1998-2004 and 2023; every
  other year unchanged.
