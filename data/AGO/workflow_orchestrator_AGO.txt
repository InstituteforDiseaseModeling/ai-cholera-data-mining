3. WHY 99.7% IS WEAKER THAN IT LOOKS - THE REAL TARGETS

handful of long rows carries a lot of it. The weekly series MOSAIC actually
consumes is built from NATIONAL rows only, priority WHO > JHU > AI; for AI rows
a <=7-day row is "observed", longer rows are Fourier-spread, and when several AI
rows overlap a week the HIGHER sCh wins.
