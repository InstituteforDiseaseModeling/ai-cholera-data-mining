4. What the 99.9% hides - read before using the data
   - Coverage counts any month touched by an observation. JHU supplies 516 months, but its national series is annual-only
     for most years before 2013, and the weekly builder (py/build_weekly_timeseries.py) ranks WHO > JHU > AI regardless of
     method. So AI national rows reach only 645 of the 2,962 weeks of the model series: 1970-1977 (zero), 1986-1989
     (counts), 2025-W03/W04 (WHO explicit zeros) and 2026-05-25..09-20. Most AI sub-annual timing never reaches the model.
   - Sub-annual timing is still unknown (no dated count found) for: 1989 Oct-Dec; 1990 Jun-Dec; 1992 Jun-Dec; 1993 Jan-Oct
     (HMIS lower bounds only); Nov 1993-Jan 1994 (routine surveillance collapsed: non-reporting, no zero permitted);
     2001 May-Dec; 2002 Jan-May and Nov-Dec; 2006 Mar-Jul; 2007 Mar-Oct; 2008 Mar-Jun and Aug-Oct; 2009 Mar-Jun and Aug-Dec;
     2022 Apr-Nov. Agent 6 judged several of these "probable true absence", but with no explicit dated statement they are
     left empty. Assessment: the monthly counts probably exist only in MoH EPISTAT/HMIS paper returns and are not online;
     more web searching is unlikely to find them - MoH archive or IDSR database access is needed.
   - 2026-09-21..10-03 is a reporting lag (WHO dashboard ends epiwk 37). District counts for May-Sep 2026 are unpublished
     (last public MoH district table: 13 Apr 2026). Sub-national counts 1979-1989 do not exist online (presence only).
   - Baseline totals that AI evidence contradicts still outrank AI in the model: JHU 1992 = 479 (MMWR 1,044 Feb-May 1992),
     1998 = 1,067 (MoH EPISTAT 3,137; UN 1,400 Jan-Aug), 2007 = 365 (>=436 sub-national), 2012 = 214 (MoH >=284), and 2020
     = 277 against five sources at 95-139. The JHU 1978 annual is spread into the 20 weeks before the documented 25 May
     onset (2,173 cases), and four 2024 weeks that WHO records as 0 receive ~22-63 JHU-derived cases each (Appendix L).
