2. WHAT THIS CYCLE FOUND
- 2024 was a real epidemic year that WHO recorded as zero. Benin's own Annuaire des
  statistiques sanitaires 2024 (SIMR table 122; metadata 306, recovered from WAHO's
  ROADIS repository) records 1,906 suspected / 205 confirmed / 26 deaths. WHO's WER
  "Cholera, 2024" lists Benin as zero-reporting, and the WHO weekly AWD file, AFRO
  bulletins, Africa CDC and ECDC all missed it. Added: department and health-zone
  layer (rows 1269-1301, 33/33 re-verified against the PDF). Held back: the national
  and Littoral (1,060/129/21) totals, which exceed 1,000 cases with a single source.
  Jul-Dec 2024 recorded as presence (about 1,511 undated cases implied by 1,906 minus
  UNICEF's 395 to 7 Jul).
- 2022-2024 national: MoH/GTFCC 279 suspected / 10 confirmed / 1 death for Jun 2023-
  Jun 2024 (row 657) and about 12 cases for Jun 2022-Jun 2023 (row 793).
- 2021-22 wave: WHO AFRO weekly-bulletin national series at 1-2 week resolution,
  Nov 2021-Apr 2022 (rows 987-999); Annuaire 2021 department and zone layers
  (national 1,248/50/23; 36/36 rows re-verified).
- 2011-2021: first monthly national series for 2011 (rows 1255-1266, 775 cases);
  UNICEF/RCP weekly national series 2012-2021 (rows 676-792).
- 2004-2013: department and Cotonou arrondissement/quarter series from the Fousseni
  2016 thesis (the largest single source, 143 live rows; 81/81 checked).
- 1991 epidemic (7,474 cases, Benin's largest): monthly national series summing exactly
  to WHO's total and the first sub-national counts (departments, sous-prefectures,
  Cotonou districts) from Foundohou et al., Med Trop 1993 (45/45 checked); WHO's
  balancing notification 2,630/53 (WER 1992;67(7)).
- 1970-74: WHO's retrospective department-week series for the first pandemic wave
  (WER 1971;46(20), 43/43 checked); 1972 department-weeks; 1974 hospital counts
  showing WHO's 73 is a floor, not a total.
- 2000 and 2002 department rows from the INSAE yearbook are FACILITY DIAGNOSES, not
  notified cases (rows 1223-1238, weight 0.3-0.5, non-primary to the national totals).

8. DOWNSTREAM CAVEATS FOR MOSAIC
- py/build_weekly_timeseries.py ranks JHU above AI. Agent 7's exact replica of the
  builder shows JHU supplies 2,498 national weeks and AI 462; only 16 AI rows win any
  week and only two of those carry cases. In years with a JHU annual row, the AI
  layer's weekly and sub-national detail does not reach the national series.
- Consequently these JHU defects leak through: the "WHO Annual" 2024 zero and UNICEF
  weekly zeros for 8 Jul-29 Sep 2024 (2024 will look like a 395-case year, not 1,906);
  the JHU 2020 weekly stream summing to 62 against a true 224-232; a JHU "2021 week
  1" layer that is really the 2020 annual commune totals; JHU 2013 weeks one ISO week
  early; and 2022-2023 sex/age splits stored as extra national totals. The gap analyser
  also counts Oct-Dec 2024 as covered because of the false JHU zero.
