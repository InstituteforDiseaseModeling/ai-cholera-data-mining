What was added. This cycle took the AI layer from 25 rows / 15 sources to 668 rows / 222 sources:
679 rows written (Agents 1-6: 674; Agent 7: 5) and 36 retracted (all tombstoned, none lost). Of the
668 live rows, 372 carry cases or deaths and 296 are zero-transmission rows (289 Documented_Absence,
7 Inferred_Absence). Sources: 190 Level 1 (WHO WER/WHSR, Ministry of Health yearbooks, INSD, peer-reviewed),
24 Level 2 (UNICEF, IFRC, US State Dept cables, theses, UN), 8 Level 3 (JPRS radio/press translations, IPS,
Xinhua), 0 Level 4. 605 of 668 rows rest on Level 1 sources. 940 search queries were run by Agents 1-6.

What it changed. Against the JHU baseline alone (there is no WHO file for BFA), monthly coverage rises from
58.1% (396/682 months, 11 gap periods) to 99.9% (681/682; only the 3-day-old October 2026 is missing). That
headline is inflated by long zero rows, so the honest measure is STRICT coverage (counts plus documented
absences only): 58.1% -> 91.5% (624/682). Within this cycle strict coverage went from 67.7% to 91.5% and
months with positive cases from 270 to 312. The AI layer is the only source for 284 country-months (227 strict).
Of the 11 baseline gap periods, 8 are now closed by counts or documented absence (1986-90, 1992-94, 1997,
2002, 2004, 2007, 2009-10, 2013), 3 are only partly closed (1976-77, 1979-83, 2025-26), and none is untouched.
By days: 63% of the gap days are documented absence, 17% have counts, and 20% rest on inferred absence alone.

Key gaps filled.

   1970-74 El Tor wave down to cercle level (Dori, Djibo, Oudalan, Nouna, Tougan, Titao, Ouagadougou ...),
   1975, 1978, 1984-85, 1991, 1995-96, 1998-2001, 2003, 2005 (incl. the MoH/DLM weekly curve, weeks 32-41,
   summing exactly to 1050) and 2012 district series (Dori, Gorom-Gorom, Sebba).
 - No-JHU years: 1990 (7 cases) and 1992-94 (33, 36, 8 cases) from the MoH 'Annuaire serie longue' Tableau
   56 replace inferred zeros that the primary national series contradicts; documented absence for 1976,
   1979, 1983, 1986-89, 1997, 2002, 2004, 2007, 2009-10, 2013 and 2025; documented corroboration (with
   surveillance status) of the JHU zeros of 2006, 2008, 2014-2020 and 2023-24.
 - Sub-national absence: 257 zero rows (210 region-year, 47 province/district/cercle), mostly from MoH yearbook
   district tables (2005, 2008-17, 2020-25), plus WER cercle and 2012 UNICEF/IFRC weekly zeros. Agent 6
   audited the 2013-2025 region-years for conflict-era surveillance: 18 disrupted, 16 partial, rest operational.
 - Five definitively wrong pre-cycle rows removed: a Cameroon outbreak (12,517 cases) filed as Burkina 2021-22
   (x2), a UNICEF 2025 figure absent from its own source, and two multi-year zeros (1975-1990, 2002-2004)
   contradicted by WHO tables.

Data quality. The validator passes with ERROR 0 (start ERROR 0 / WARN 49; end ERROR 0 / WARN 48). The 47 high-
CFR warnings are genuine pre-ORS lethalities (1970-75 El Tor, 1984-85, 1991, 1996), all capped at <= 0.7;
the remaining warning is the Dori index case of 17 Jun 1984, a real single-day event. All 222 source URLs
resolve to the document or to an archived copy. In a stratified random sample of 46 rows (all 14 Level 3 rows,
24 rows over 100 cases) the figures were confirmed in the source for 46/46; 42/46 quotes are verbatim or exact
table transcriptions; the 4 that are not (Idx 1, 2, 11, 19) are pre-cycle rows whose quotes are paraphrased or
translated - the 12 such legacy rows now carry the exact source wording. Every row over 1000 cases has at least
two independent sources. Overall: HIGH for 1970-75, 1984-85, 2005 and 2008-18 (Level 1 tables, cross-
validated); MODERATE for 1986-2004, where the only national series (MoH DLM) undercounts WHO in most epidemic
years and prints 0 for missing data, and for 2019-26 (conflict-region surveillance partial or disrupted; 2026
rests on list omission); LOW for 1977 and 1980-82 (inferred absence only).

Remaining limitations - the disappointing numbers.
 - Model impact is far smaller than the row count. py/build_weekly_timeseries.py uses national rows only and
   lets any JHU national row outrank AI for that week, so of 141 national AI rows only 26 can reach the
   national weekly series (4 of them with cases: 1990, 1992, 1993, 1994); 115 are overridden by JHU annual or
   weekly rows, and the 527 sub-national rows are not used at all. The 1970s cercle weeks and the 2005 weekly
   curve also never reach the seasonal template, which is built from JHU/WHO weekly rows only.
 - Still inferred, never documented: 1977, 1980, 1981, 1982 (WHO non-notification only; no national cholera
   table exists online) and 2026-01-01..09-28 (Africa CDC/ECDC list omission). 2026-09-29..10-03 is open:
   nothing has been published.
 - Agent 6 added 0 rows (3 batches at 0%); its output was classification only.
 - No sub-national counts exist for 1975, 1978, 1995-2000 or 2003 (presence records only, and the 1999-2000 ones
   are list carry-over), nor for the 163 cases of 17 Sep-14 Oct 2001 outside Ouargaye; the 617-case epidemic
   of Jan-May 2000 has no known location at all.
 - The 1992-94 counts rest on one MoH table that WHO never received; their deaths are unknown. The zeros for
   1997 and 2004 are contested (confidence 0.5).
 - From 2019 the region-level zeros come from a shrinking network (Sahel's weekly reports halved 2018-2024;
   Djibo at 8.8% of 2018 in 2025) whose completeness percentages hide the closed facilities.
 Does the data exist? For 1977/1980-82 and the 1990s sub-national detail: probably on paper (MoH DLM reports,
 Centre Muraz/OCCGE activity reports, Med Afr Noire 1987) but not online. For 2026: yes, once the MoH 2026
 yearbook and WER annual report appear (expected mid/late 2027).

PIPELINE NOTES (for the maintainers)
======================================================================================================
(i)  WER weeks start on Sunday, but py/build_weekly_timeseries.py files every row of <= 7 days under the ISO
     week of its TL (r["tl"].isocalendar()). A WER week such as 10-16 VI 1973 (Sun-Sat) therefore lands in the
     ISO week ending on its first day - one ISO week early - for every 1970s cercle row and IHR notification.
(ii) JHU annual rows outrank AI rows for every ISO week they cover (try_insert, priority WHO > JHU > AI), and the
     seasonal template is built only from JHU/WHO weekly rows. AI intra-year detail for JHU-annual years
     (1970-75, 1978, 1984-85, 1991, 1995-2001, 2003, 2005, 2011-12) therefore never reaches the national weekly
     series or the template. Side effect: in JHU zero years (2006, 2008, 2018-19, 2023-24) a JHU annual zero,
     capped at inferred_zero 0.6, replaces AI documented zeros of 0.85-0.95.
(iii) Same-period conflicts within one source resolve to the LARGER count (same method rank, then higher sCh),
     biasing the series upward unless the smaller, final value is enforced by retracting the other row.
(iv) There is no verb to edit or remove presence rows (./data/{ISO}/cholera_presence_ai.csv) and none to rewrite
     the 'Surveillance system status during absence' token (revise_observation.py --set-evidence covers only the
     Evidence type). Nor can revise_observation.py blank a field (--deaths "" means 'no change'); Agent 7 had to
     retract and re-add 484-486 to clear printed-but-unknown deaths.
(v)  The 2005 MoH/DLM weekly rows (Idx 688-699, Kyelem 2011 Fig. 1) assume ISO epidemiological weeks. This is
     supported (weeks 32-35 = 8 Aug-4 Sep match WHO DON 23 Sep 2005, 615 vs 617 cases; week 32 contains the
     12 Aug onset); another convention would shift the dates by at most a few days.
