APPENDIX B - BASELINE AND BUILDER DEFECTS FOR HUMAN FOLLOW-UP (not edited here)
================================================================================
B1. 2016 weekly series = 364 cases/7 deaths, against 265/20 (MoH, WHO, IFRC).
    - Cause: try_insert in py/build_weekly_timeseries.py keeps the higher sCh
      when same-source rows share a week.
    - JHU UID 3180 (correct dates) and UID 5104 (the same curve one week early)
      are both "JHU observed", so each week takes max(A[w], A[w-1]). The result
      is +99 cases (+37%); deaths come mostly from 5104, which records none.
    - CORRECTION to the hand-off framing: the eight UID 3180 age/sex strata (sum
      266, Primary=False) ARE stored as national rows, because the converter
      drops the sex and age columns. They would inflate any naive sum of
      national rows, but the builder skips them under its 50% weekly-coverage
      rule.
B2. 2013 surveillance gap -> assumed_zero (0.5).
    - The builder has no surveillance-gap status, so Agent 6's retraction of
      Index 55 (0.4) raised the zero weight for 2013.
    - The runner's last CSV (./data/CAF/cholera_weekly_CAF.csv, 22:06) predates
      the retraction.
B3. Source outranks evidence.
    - JHU annuals for 1997 (443/75), 2011 (117/15) and 2012 (21/0) are
      Fourier-spread over 52 weeks at 0.5. The AI-dated series lose: 2011 was
      confined to weeks 38-42 (16, 49, 94, 12, 1).
    - JHU UID 5104 regional-platform zeros (2013-12-30..2019-04-14) render as
      observed at 0.9. HDX shows CAR = 0 for every week of 2012, when WHO AFRO
      reported 21 cases, so these zeros are default fills.
B4. JHU UID 5104, 2020-01-06..11-08: 44 weeks are Phantom=True upstream
    (../jhu_cholera_data/data/Central African Republic/ObservationCollection5104.csv).
    The flag survives only as text in processing_notes; the builder renders
    these weeks as observed zeros at 0.63.
B5. JHU 2005-2006 zeros are WHO roster defaults, not CAR zero reports (Agents
    3/6). They render as inferred zeros at 0.6, which is acceptable.
B6. JHU 2004 = 320/48 is unresolved and probably an artefact (Section 4c).
    Modellers should down-weight it.
B7. WHO baseline ./data/CAF/cholera_data_who.csv
    - It ends 2026-08-02 and records epi-week 30 as 10/1. The live WHO service
      and ../ees-cholera-mapping (2026-10-01) give 8/0 and add weeks 31-35.
    - CAR is NOT affected by the 2026 WHO week-shift defect: epi-week 16 is
      2026-04-20 in both.
B8. Tooling
    - The repair tool's duplicate_merge writes no tombstone. It also recorded an
      unchecked "cross-validation" when it merged Index 7.
    - No tool can revise presence records. The presence record for Bangui 3
      (2026-06-26..07-15, from OCHA/Xinhua "all districts of the capital") is
      contradicted by WHO's four-district list and by the 18 Sep government
      statement. It needs human review.
