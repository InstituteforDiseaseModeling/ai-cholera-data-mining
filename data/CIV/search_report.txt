2. KEY FINDINGS
(a) 1972-1990 and 1997-1999 - UNDER-NOTIFIED, NOT ABSENT. The IPCI V. cholerae O1 strain register (Bai 1991 annex) gives
    1972 105, 1977 43, 1978 2, 1981 5, 1982 45, 1983 155, 1984 2, 1985 9, 1986 18, 1987 1, 1988 67, 1989 79, 1990 12
    isolates. WHO tables list CIV only in 1979 (3) and 1982 (34, "incomplete"). CHU de Cocody isolated 3 in 1975 and 1 in
    1980, against a register 0 in both years. Other events: Tortiya Nov 1984 145 cases / 31 deaths; MACA prison Sep 1991
    ~50 deaths and Jul 1997 44 deaths; routine HMIS 88 cases (1997) and 16 (1998); 1999 >=19 confirmed isolates. The IHR
    channel worked for yellow fever, polio, measles and dracunculiasis, so the silence is specific to cholera. Zero rows
    remain only for 1974, 1976, Jan-Apr 1977, Jan-Oct 1978, Jan-25 Nov 1981 and Jan-14 May 1985 (inferred, 0.2-0.6),
    plus a WER-documented zero for Sep 1979.
(b) 2002-2007 PARTITION: the north and west were a SURVEILLANCE GAP from Sep 2002 to 2003. The MoH bulletin "only
    covers the government controlled zone, leaving out 40% of the country"; INHP promptness fell to 25% at the crisis
    peak. 2004-2006 was partial (NGO detection) and Jan-Mar 2007 was a gap. National 2002-2004 counts are
    government-zone lower bounds.
(c) 2011 POST-ELECTORAL BLACKOUT: the INHP case database has no notification in ISO weeks S11-S20 (about 26 Mar-15 May
    2011); UNICEF wrote that "surveillance systems are down". The period is left without rows, not zeros.
(d) 2016-2024 TRUE QUIET WITH OPERATIONAL SURVEILLANCE: suspected cases were detected and tested every year and all were
    laboratory-negative (2017 31/0 ... 2021 12 tested/0 positive ... 2024 5/0). DHIS2 completeness was 98.5-100%, and
    Abidjan sewage in 2023-24 yielded only non-O1, ctx-negative V. cholerae.
(e) 2025 EPIDEMIC resolved at 556 cases / 123 confirmed / 24 deaths, where the WHO annual report (WER 101(38)) equals
    the INHP final figure. Onset 25 May 2025 at Vridi-Akobrakre; six districts affected; end declared from 14 Dec 2025.
(f) 2026: documented absence 1 Jan-5 Jul (INHP Vigile N931, 0.95); probable absence 6 Jul-20 Sep from comprehensive
    WHO and Africa CDC exclusion lists (0.6/0.5); the Ghana "outbreak" signal was a prevention story.

5. BUILDER / BASELINE DEFECTS - read before modelling (simulation of py/build_weekly_timeseries.py; not modified)
- assumed_zero: 112 weeks have no row in any layer and are written as sCh=0: all of 1973, 48 weeks of 1983, Aug and Dec
  1977, Dec 1978. Cholera was present or possible in each. MODEL THEM AS MISSING, NOT ZERO.
- 1970: the JHU annual 868 is Fourier-spread over the whole year, so 699 cases land before cholera arrived on 20 Oct 1970.
  JHU outranks the AI documented absence row 815.
- 2012: JHU "2012-01-01..03-15 1321/34" is a misdated cumulative. The series gets 1,341 cases in Q1 2012, against a true
  64-69 (WHO AFRO), and 1,772 for the year against 424 (WHO) / 431 (INHP) / 589 (RASS). The AI platform weekly curve
  (411) is overridden.
- 2006: JHU "2006-01-01..08-28 321/2" is the Feb 2006 Zouan-Hounien focus spread over eight months (series 436, WHO 414).
- 2015-2018: JHU "UNICEF Country Programme Report (UID 5108)" rows mix weekly counts with cumulative-from-January
  snapshots. The file sums to 112 / 468 / 151 for 2016-2018 against weekly totals of 16 / 24 / 10. The builder's
  50%-coverage skip protects most years (2017 renders 29), but 2015 renders 131 against a national 199-200.
- 2025: WHO reporting-date batches (W23 45, W26 55, W27 9, W29 280, W33 114, with zeros in W24-25, W28 and W30-32) outrank
  the INHP onset curve, so peak onset weeks with 89 and 75 cases render as 0. AI onset weeks S21-S22 are added on top of
  WHO's W23 report of the same cases, giving 586 against 556.
- WHO week numbering: the dashboard's 2025 W53 starts 29 Dec 2025, so WHO 2026 week N = ISO week N+1. This is the known
  baseline-converter week-shift bug. CIV has no 2026 WHO rows, so CIV is not directly affected.
- Annual rows whose ISO week 1 starts in December spill into the next year: 1997-W01 gets 27 cases of JHU 1996.
  Annual laboratory-register rows are spread flat (the K=1 template peaks in March), although for example the 1988
  epidemic began in October.
- Fix suggested: let an observed AI weekly row outrank a Fourier-spread JHU/WHO aggregate; correct or drop JHU UID 901
  (2012) and 902 (2006); de-duplicate the UID 5108 snapshots in the JHU converter; start the JHU 1970 row on
  1970-10-16; treat assumed_zero as missing in MOSAIC.
