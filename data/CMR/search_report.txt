EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
BOTTOM LINE.
- The AI layer for Cameroon went from 22 rows (20 of them unverified reconstructions) to 1,114 rows, of which
  1,112 were added this cycle. In a 69-row source re-check, every row whose source could be reached matched it.
- Headline coverage rose from 96.2% to 99.9% of months, but that figure overstates the gain: the baseline was
  already high because of one JHU annual national row per year.
- The real contribution is resolution:
  * national data at quarterly or finer resolution doubled (133 -> 271 months), including the first sub-annual
    national series for 1970-2009;
  * sub-national data now exist for 1971-2009 and 2023-2026, where the baseline had none.
- Two national gaps remain open: 1977 (non-reporting; only a Douala figure exists) and 21 Sep-4 Oct 2026
  (reporting lag in an active outbreak).
- Much of the new national detail does not yet reach the weekly model input, because of how the builder ranks
  sources.
