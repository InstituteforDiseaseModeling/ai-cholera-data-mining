      adding a province-week in 2015-2021).

      Derive an increment by subtraction only between two cumulative figures of the SAME series, and
      say so ("derived by subtraction: cumulative X at A minus Y at B"). Do not leave a cumulative row

      caps confidence at 0.7) and add context.

      weight accordingly (protocol section 6). Record both numbers when sources disagree; never average.
