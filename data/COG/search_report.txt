EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
What exists now. ./data/COG/cholera_data_ai.csv holds 611 observations (479 with case counts, 132 validated
zero periods) citing 188 sources in ./data/COG/metadata_ai.csv (125 Level 1, 48 Level 2, 15 Level 3, 0 Level 4).
A further 27 "cholera present, no count" entries sit in ./data/COG/cholera_presence_ai.csv and 26 withdrawn rows
are kept as tombstones in ./data/COG/retracted_rows_ai.csv. This cycle took the layer from 164 rows / 70 sources to
611 rows / 188 sources. Geography: 263 national rows, 177 department (ADM1), 157 district, 14 health-area or
locality rows.

What the AI layer added. JHU + WHO baselines alone cover 46.3% of the 681 months from January 1970 to September
2026 (315 months, 6 gap periods). With the AI layer the figure is 99.0% (674 months, 2 gap periods). For 357 of those
months the AI layer is the only source. The biggest additions:
  - 1970-1977 and 1980-1996 (25 years with no baseline data) are now documented as probable absences: 25 annual
    Inferred_Absence rows, checked against every issue of the Weekly Epidemiological Record (WER) for 1970-1996.
    For this audit I read 1,064 full issues myself (1970-77, 1981-92, 1995); Agent 4 read the other years.
    No Congo cholera notification turned up.
  - Sub-annual national series for the epidemics: 1978 and 1979 (week level), 1997-99, 2006-07, 2008, 2009, 2011
    (MoH weekly table, 31 weeks, sums to the MoH total of 775), 2012-13 (platform weekly), 2018, 2023, and the 2025-26
    epidemic (MoH SITREP-interval series from the 21 Jun 2025 index case to 6 Sep 2026, plus a zero week for 7-13 Sep).
  - Department and district detail for 2011, 2012-13, 2023 and 2025-26. The 2025-26 district layer reconciles exactly
    with the MoH SITREP N49 total (1,707) and with the WHO AFRO week-36 department totals, except for the 2 cases
    already recorded as conflicts on Index 190 and 191.
  - Documented zero windows between epidemics: 2002-05, 2010, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2021-22, 2023 and 2024.

What is still open (the disappointing part):
  1. 2001-01-01..2001-06-30 (181 days): no count and no nil statement exists in any public source. Five agents
     searched it. The only lead is one uncited sentence in the WHO Country Cooperation Strategy 2009-13, which names
     "2001" epidemics in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire. Kinshasa had cholera across the river until June 2001, and
     reporting to WHO was passive. The period stays empty, with a 0.4 presence entry. The data most likely exist only
     in offline archives.
  2. 2026-09-14..2026-09-30 (17 days): a reporting lag in an active epidemic tail. The WHO dashboard ends 13 Sep
     (last edited 28 Sep). The newest Africa CDC issue is dated 13 Sep. There is no WHO AFRO bulletin after week 36,
     no MoH SITREP after N49, and IFRC MDRCG028 has only its April document. No row or zero was written. This gap
     will fill once Congo reports.
  3. Some periods are left empty on purpose because nobody was reporting: 2019-11-25..2020-12-31 (WHO: Congo did not
     report cholera data for 2020), Jan-Oct 1997 (war), Sep-Dec 1998, H2 1999, May-Dec 2000, and Jun-Dec 2009.
     JHU zeros or annual totals cover them in the weekly series; the AI layer does not.
  4. Agent 6 produced 0 rows from 120 queries (6 batches, including a targeted redeployment). Its contribution was
     the gap classification and 13 relabels or re-weights, not data.

Data quality. The validator (python py/validate_quality.py COG) passes with ERROR 0 / WARN 10 / INFO 216. All 10
WARNs are CFR above 15%. Each row has a same-denominator note and sits at the 0.7 cap: the 1997-98 epidemic during
the war, epidemic tails with small counts, and riverine windows where community deaths were validated after the fact.
They are left visible, not suppressed. Structural checks:
  - Dual-reference integrity, date order, CFR arithmetic, confidence ranges, location codes and zero-row evidence
    labels are 100% correct.
  - No row is above its source's reliability band.
  - All 421 overlapping same-location pairs have a declared primary row.
  - 187 of 188 source URLs are live or archived. The one dead URL was already marked Superseded.

What this audit changed (0 rows added, 0 sources added, 0 WebSearch calls; all retrieval direct):
  - Retracted Index 614 (Brazzaville 2018, 6 cases). It was a redundant copy of JHU rows 6744/6747 from the same
    platform.
  - Revised Index 314: Ile Mbamou deaths 13 -> 12. MoH SITREP N08 -> N09 shows one death moved from Ile Mbamou to
    Talangai (Index 86), so the district layer was counting it twice. It now matches the national total of 67 deaths.
  - Relabelled three platform-only zeros from Documented_Absence to Inferred_Absence and lowered their weights:
    329 (2013 H2, 0.7 -> 0.6), 332 (Sep 2012, 0.6 -> 0.5) and 554 (2013 week 24, 0.6 -> 0.4). The 2012 and 2013
    platform workbooks contain no NA cells at all, so their zeros may be defaults. Index 554 also conflicts with the
    IFRC count of 19 June cases.
  - Confirmed all 13 of Agent 6's relabels and re-weights. Kept Index 629 (WHO dashboard zero week, 7-13 Sep 2026)
    after checking that the reporting-period layer is set per country. Kept 594-604 and 621 as confirmation rows.
    Kept all 11 low-weight yearbook rows.
  - Appended audit notes to 75 rows, including corrected pointers where notes still referred to retracted rows.

Warning for modellers. The weekly series that MOSAIC reads (py/build_weekly_timeseries.py) ranks WHO > JHU > AI, so
several JHU baseline defects outrank corrected AI rows. In a sandbox build on 2026-09-30:
  - 2018 comes out at 510 cases, against the platform's final 67 and WHO's 60.
  - 2019 comes out at 67 cases where it should be 0.
  - 2013 comes out at 1,630 cases, driven by the WER total of 1,624 with 221 deaths, which looks wrong.
  - 2023 comes out at 205 cases, against the MoH total of 71.
  - The 2014-16 platform default zeros enter as 0.9-confidence observed weeks.
No AI edit can fix these (see Appendix I, P1-P3). They distort the modelled Congo series more than anything that is
still left to collect.

       In a sandbox build (unmodified builder on copies of the three layer files, 2026-09-30):
         2018 = 510: JHU holds the platform series twice (5900-5937 correct; 5120-5148 shifted 10 weeks early) plus
           running cumulatives entered as weekly values (5157-5168). The platform final is 67 and WHO's is 60.
         2019 = 67: JHU 5223 carries the 2018 total to 2019-10-21.
         2013 = 1,630: JHU/WER 1,624/221 (judged erroneous).
         2023 = 205: six overlapping JHU 2023 annual rows, with the builder taking the maximum per week, against the MoH
           total of 71.
         2011 = 868, against the MoH total of 775.
         1979 = 5, against Yala 1982's 14.
       AI sub-annual national rows for 1978-79, 1997-2000, 2006-09 and 2011-13 do not reach the series while JHU annual
       rows exist.
   P1b JHU zeros sourced 'UNICEF Country Programme Report (UID 5106)' for 2014-2016 and 2019-11..2020 enter as JHU
       weekly 'observed' zeros at 0.9. They outrank the AI Inferred rows (0.5-0.6), although WHO AFRO says Congo sent no
       cholera data in 2015 H1 and no IDSR data in 2016 H1, and WER 2020 says Congo did not report.
   P2  The builder ignores NON-PRIMARY flags and keeps the per-week maximum among overlapping same-source rows.
       NON-PRIMARY Index 16 adds 62.8 cases to 2026 (weeks W06 and W09). The builder's 50%-coverage rule drops Index 580
       (91 cases) entirely because one of its two ISO weeks holds an AI weekly row.
   P3  ./data/COG/cholera_data_who.csv ends 2026-05-10, while the live dashboard runs to 13 Sep 2026 (last edited
       28 Sep). Its 2026 weeks come from an older dashboard vintage and differ from the live layer in 14 of 18 weeks
       (e.g. 9 Feb 11 vs 66; 2 Mar 91 vs 42). Weeks 1-23 of 2025 exist but are blank. Zero weeks are stored as blank
       sCh (all 53 weeks of 2024 are blank), so the builder treats them as missing. The file needs a refresh; note the
       known convert_who_to_workflow.py append bug.
   P4  Index-reuse defect in py/add_observation.py (fixed 2026-09-30): 32 historical collisions (AGO 8, COG 6, ETH 1,
       GHA 10, MRT 1, NGA 4, RWA 2). In COG, tombstones 624-629 (2018 department rows withdrawn by Agent 5) share
       numbers with live rows 624-629 (Aug 2025 district splits and the 7-13 Sep 2026 zero). Each live row discloses the
       reuse; the numbers were not renumbered.
   P5  There is no edit or delete verb for presence rows. The 2001 full-year presence entry should read
       2001-01-01..06-30 because Index 544 holds H2. There is also no verb for metadata Validation_Status (177 of 188
       'Pending') or for URLs.
   P6  The dashboard's 2026 epi-week numbering lags ISO by one week (date_wk 2025-12-29 = epiwk 53; 2026-01-05 = epiwk
       1; 2026-09-07 = epiwk 36 = ISO W37). Converters must key on date_wk.
   P7  Cross-layer location names: the MoH now uses its 2024 departments. JHU writes 'Cuvette Ouest', 'CG-16' and
       regex-like strings such as '(Brazzaville|Likouala)'. Sub-national joins need a crosswalk.

       phrases, and the CFR-context regex misses wording like 'shares one denominator'. Primacy therefore had to be
       audited separately (/tmp/COG_a7/overlap_audit.py).
   P9  ReliefWeb (43 source URLs) and HDX now return an AWS-WAF JavaScript challenge (HTTP 202), and the ReliefWeb API
