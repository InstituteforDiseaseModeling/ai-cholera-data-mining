** THE STRONGEST NEGATIVE AVAILABLE FOR 1998-2002, AND WHY IT STILL IS NOT A ROW **
The IDMC/NRC displacement profile (metadata index 38) is not a secondary summary -
it is a compilation of VERBATIM QUOTED field reporting from the Information
Coordination Centre (OCHA/ERREC), MSF-France, MSF-Holland, OXFAM, Save the
Children, UNICEF, WHO and the GOE/UN appeal team, camp by camp, across 1998-2002.
It covers 24 organised camps in Debub, Gash-Barka and Northern Red Sea holding
208,163 residents. It names the diarrhoeal diseases that DID occur, specifically
and by aetiology:
  - Mai-habar camp: "The water and sanitation situation has been fragile in this
    camp (resulting, amongst others, in an OUTBREAK OF SHIGGELOSIS)"
  - Harena: "reported incidences of shigelosis"; Alba camp: "Diarrhea has become
    the number one source of morbidity"
  - Buya camp, MSF-F visit 20 Nov 2000: "Common diseases among the IDP population
    include bloody diarrhoea, acute respiratory problems, anaemia and suspected TB"
  - MSF-F mobile teams Aug 2000: "increased incidence rate of diarrhea and
    dysentery, as well as acute respiratory infections ... and malaria"
  - End-2000 national health status: "Diarrhea and acute respiratory tract and eye
    infections are common"; WHO 2001: "high rates of infectious diseases such as
    diarrhoea, malaria, acute respiratory infection, conjunctivitis and skin
    diseases ... the risk of epidemics remains high"
"Cholera" occurs ZERO times in all 352,122 characters.
