CONFLICT 5 - VERDICT: CONFIRMED, AND SLIGHTLY STRONGER THAN STATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rebaudet S, Sudre B, Faucher B, Piarroux R. "Cholera in Coastal Africa: A
Systematic Review of Its Heterogeneous Environmental Determinants." J Infect Dis
2013;208(Suppl 1):S98-S106. Table 1 read directly from the PDF:

  Table heading : "Table 1.  Cholera Cases Notified by African Coastal Countries"
  Eritrea row   : Total cases 1970-2011 = 120 ; Total cases 2002-2011 = 120
  Footnote b    : "Notification data extracted from the WHO yearly cholera
                   overviews [1]."
  Abbreviation  : "ND, no data."

Three things follow, and the third is new.
 (i)  120 - 120 = 0 notified cases in 1970-2001, and 120 = 119 (2007) + 1 (2008)
      exactly per WHO GHO. The series is pure WHO notification.
 (ii) The word in the heading and in footnote b is NOTIFIED / notification. The
      zero is a zero of REPORTING. Confirmed verbatim.
 (iii) NEW - the table distinguishes a true zero from no data and Eritrea is
      neither. Mauritius is printed as "0" and Egypt and Libya are printed
      BLANK, with "ND, no data" defined in the abbreviations. So these authors
      did have a convention for "reported zero" and a convention for "no data".
      Eritrea is given a positive total (120), so its 1970-2001 zero is NOT a
      printed cell at all - it is arithmetic on two printed cells. Rebaudet
      nowhere asserts that Eritrea reported zero in 1970-2001.
 (iv) NEW - Eritrea did not exist as a state until 1993 and could not notify WHO
      independently before then. Any "Eritrea 1970-1992" figure in a WHO country
      series is structurally blank, not zero.
AUTHORITATIVE BOUND ON THE HISTORICAL GAP: Eritrea notified WHO of no cholera
between 1970 and 2001. That is a statement about WHO's inbox. It licenses no
zero row anywhere in 1971-2004, exactly as Agents 1-4 concluded.
