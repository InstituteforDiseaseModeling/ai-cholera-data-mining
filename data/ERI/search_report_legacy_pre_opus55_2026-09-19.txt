D2. THE WEEKLY BUILDER DISCARDS BOTH AI-DISCOVERED ERITREAN OUTBREAKS.
NEW FINDING, and the most consequential one in this report. In
py/build_weekly_timeseries.py, SOURCE_PRIORITY = {"WHO": 3, "JHU": 2, "AI": 1}
ranks JHU above AI unconditionally, and load_national_rows() reads only rows
whose Location is exactly "AFR::{iso}". Consequently:
  - Index 19 (Tessenei 2006: 60 cases, 3 deaths, lab-confirmed, weight 0.85)
    is sub-national, so it never enters the series at all.
  - Index 10, 20, 21, 22 (2016) are sub-national - excluded for the same reason.
  - Index 11 (2016 national, 23 deaths) IS national but has a blank sCh, so
    has_count is False and the row is skipped by design.
The result: ./data/ERI/cholera_weekly_ERI.csv reports
  2006 weeks 38-44 -> sCh 0.0, deaths 0.0, JHU, inferred_zero
  2016 weeks 34-39 -> sCh 0.0, deaths 0.0, JHU, inferred_zero
i.e. the model is told those weeks were cholera-free while this file documents
60 lab-confirmed cases and 23 deaths inside them. The ENTIRE ERI weekly series
contains ZERO positive weeks from the AI layer; its only 105 non-zero weeks are
JHU's Fourier-disaggregated 2007-2009 annual totals.

The AI layer's three best-evidenced absence rows are also overwritten: Index 4
(WHO AFRO Q2 2015 nil return, weight 0.95), Index 6 (0.65) and Index 12 (0.90)
all lose to phantom JHU zeros and are rendered as JHU/inferred_zero at 0.6. An
evidenced documented_zero is being replaced by a fabricated one.

RECOMMENDATION: make source precedence evidence-aware rather than layer-fixed -
a JHU row whose notes say "Phantom: True" should not outrank an AI
documented_zero - and give deaths-only national rows a path into the series
(currently a row with deaths=23 and blank sCh contributes nothing, though
py/analyze_effective_gaps.py does count it as a positive month, so the two
tools disagree about the same row).

D3. THE WEEKLY SERIES WAS STALE AND NOTHING DETECTED IT.

D4. THE VALIDATOR'S STALE-DEBT ADVICE IS UNSAFE UNDER SINGLE-COUNTRY SCOPING.

I removed only the two ERI entries, after verifying them against the current
file. RECOMMENDATION: suppress the stale-debt report unless the debt entry's

WHAT A FUTURE RUN SHOULD TRY THAT THIS ONE DID NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FIX THE PHANTOM-ZERO BLINDNESS FIRST, THEN RE-TARGET. Three gap periods
   (2009-2014, 2016-2019, 2021-2022) have never been searched because they do
   not appear in the gap file. Searching them is likely the highest-yield
   action available on Eritrea, and it costs nothing until the coverage metric
   is corrected. Do this before spending another query.
2. RENDER SCANNED PDFs BY DEFAULT. Any WHO/IRIS document from before ~2000 is
   likely a scan. Make `pdftotext` returning under ~500 characters trigger an
   automatic `pdftoppm -r 130 -png` pass. Index 23 was missed for ~2,000
   queries by exactly this failure, and it will be missing other countries'
   pre-2000 data right now.
3. APPLY THE POSITIVE-CONTROL RULE MECHANICALLY. Before banking an absence from
   a document, check whether a country known to have had a large outbreak that
   year is also absent from it. Metadata 67, 90, 95 and 118 were all caught
   under-covering this way - notably 118, a WHO Eritrea bulletin whose "No
   cholera ... outbreak was reported" nil template also printed during the live
   Tessenei outbreak.
4. RE-CHECK WHEN "CHOLERA, 2025" PUBLISHES. This is scheduled, cheap and is the
   only way G6 closes.
5. GENERALISE RELIEFWEB ADAPTIVE BISECTION. reliefweb.int RSS silently caps at
   20 items. Agent 5 enumerated the full country.iso3:eri slice 1994-2026 by
   requesting a year and recursively splitting any window returning exactly 20.
   This applies to all 40 countries; most have probably been under-enumerated.
6. ADD ti (Tigrinya) TO ETHIOPIA'S search_languages. ETH currently lacks it,
   and Tigray borders the Mereb basin where the 2016 Eritrean outbreak sat.
   Use SHEROKH, not KOLERA.
7. ADD A RETIREMENT PATH TO py/add_observation.py for presence records
   superseded by a data row, so item 1 under "ONE UNRESOLVED ITEM" can be
   closed without hand-editing.
8. DO NOT TRUST THE WHO CHOLERA DASHBOARD'S ZEROS. Eritrea's dashboard series
   shows 0/0 for every year 2015-2024, including years WER explicitly names
   Eritrea a non-reporter. Any agent treating a dashboard zero as a nil return
   would fabricate zero rows. This applies to all 40 countries and should be
   written into the search protocol.
