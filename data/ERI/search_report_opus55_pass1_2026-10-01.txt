BOTTOM LINE
Six agents ran 34 batches (680 queries) and turned 19 rows into 134. That
headline overstates the gain, for three reasons:
  1. 85 of the 115 new rows come from one discovery: 16 WHO Eritrea weekly

closed, and 2022 was not touched at all.
