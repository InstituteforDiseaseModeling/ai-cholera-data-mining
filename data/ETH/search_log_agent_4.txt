    EVERY ETHIOPIAN PROVINCE-LEVEL TABLE IN THE WER OF 1971-1972 IS SMALLPOX,
    NOT CHOLERA. This nearly produced ten fabricated rows and is exactly the
    column-proximity trap Agent 2 was burned by.
      - WER 1971 (10665/218443, p.324) prints, four columns to the right of a
        block headed "CHOLERA (contd.) - CHOLERA (suite)", a table reading

        Ilubabor 14 / Kaffa 117 / Shoa (excl. Addis Ababa (A)) 63 / Sidamo 282 /
        Tigre 7 / Wollo 2". Reading the column headers on the SAME page shows
        the governing heading is "SMALLPOX - VARIOLE / Africa - Afrique".
        Those are smallpox notifications for 11-24 July 1971.
      - WER 1972 (10665/218885, p.204) likewise prints "ETHIOPIA - ETHIOPIE
        30.IV-6.V / Provinces / Gamu-Gofa 199 / Kaffa 343 / Shoa 43 /
        Sidamo 176" under "SMALLPOX - VARIOLE", adjacent to Indian cholera
        districts and one line below a "CHOLERA (contd.)" continuation.
      - WER 1972 (10665/219152, p.436) contains the sentence "Presently, the
        most heavily afflicted areas are in central and north-central Ethiopia:
        Harar, Begemdir and Gojam Provinces" - which reads like a cholera
        statement but is the SMALLPOX SURVEILLANCE section (the next paragraph
        says "areas previously free from smallpox ... the ultimate progress of
        smallpox eradication on the African continent").

    because of the smallpox eradication programme, and that presence is NOT
    cholera. Any future scan of the WER 1970s corpus for "Ethiopia" must read
    the disease heading on the page, not the nearest heading in the text flow.
    NO cholera row was written from WER 1971-1972.
