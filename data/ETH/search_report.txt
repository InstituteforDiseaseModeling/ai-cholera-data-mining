2. THE GOVERNING CAVEAT - ETHIOPIAN SILENCE IS NOT ETHIOPIAN ABSENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the single most important finding for any future agent, any modeller,
and anyone tempted to "fill" Ethiopia's long gaps cheaply.

EPHI has stated in print that cholera "was not reported to the WHO due to fear
of economic repercussions." The WHO Weekly Epidemiological Record omits Ethiopia
entirely for 2015 and 2016 while EPHI's own national line list records 227 and
29,871 cases for those years. Agent 6 ran a six-disease control against WHO GHO
and found Ethiopia filed measles notifications every year from 1974 to 2010, and
filed explicit yellow-fever NIL RETURNS in many years, while its cholera line
stayed BLANK - not zero, blank.

Ethiopia's cholera line in WHO series is missing data, not reported absence.

ANY AGENT THAT INFERS ETHIOPIAN CHOLERA ABSENCE FROM WHO SILENCE WILL FABRICATE
DATA. That is precisely how the three retracted rows were created. A zero for
Ethiopia requires an affirmative statement from a source that was actually
looking - EPHI, a regional health bureau, or a field investigation - and the
distinction between "no cases reported" and "no report" must be checked in the
source text every time.

6. TWO OPEN ITEMS ADJUDICATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6a. THE JHU PHANTOM ZEROS, 2011-2014 - CONFIRMED, AND WORSE THAN A FLAG PROBLEM

./data/ETH/cholera_data_jhu.csv Index 228-231 assert sCh=0 for calendar 2011,
2012, 2013 and 2014, each annotated "Phantom: True", where Ethiopia's genuine
1982 and 2005 nil returns are "Phantom: False". The JHU baseline is read-only
and I did not touch it.

What I found:

  (1) `Phantom` IS NOT A COLUMN. py/convert_jhu_to_workflow.py line 148 reads it
      from the JHU source and line 171 embeds it in free-text processing_notes.
      It survives downstream only as prose. py/build_weekly_timeseries.py never
      references it and, as written, cannot.

  (2) There is a partial mitigation already in place. build_weekly_timeseries.py
      grades these as `inferred_zero` with confidence clamped to
      INFERRED_ZERO_MAX = 0.6 (line 428), not `documented_zero`. The code comment
      names "JHU annual zeros" explicitly. So they are not masquerading as
      documented absence.

  (3) They still assert zero. I ran process_country('ETH') in memory: all 208
      weeks of 2011-2014 emerge as sCh = 0.0, source JHU, method inferred_zero.
      2011 is contradicted by AI rows Index 555 and 556 (13 and 3 AWD cases,
      Kebri Dahar, from OCHA weekly bulletins) and by 7 presence records; WHO GHO
      has no Ethiopian cholera row at all for 2011-2014.

  (4) A SEPARATE AND MORE GENERAL DEFECT, which I verified by experiment. In
      py/build_weekly_timeseries.py, process_country() -> try_insert(), line 374:

          if SOURCE_PRIORITY[entry["source"]] > SOURCE_PRIORITY[existing["source"]]:
              merged[key] = entry

      Source priority overrides unconditionally, ignoring `method`. The
      method-rank tiebreak below it (line 378) applies only WITHIN one source,
      and the guard at line 430 (`if src_label == "JHU" and key in
      weekly_covered`) is reset per source at line 396, so it protects only
      JHU's own weekly rows. With SOURCE_PRIORITY = {"WHO":3,"JHU":2,"AI":1},
      a JHU or WHO inferred_zero at confidence 0.6 silently overwrites an AI
      `observed` positive at confidence 0.9.

      Demonstrated: injecting a synthetic AI NATIONAL observation of 500 cases
      into week 2013-W23 yields
        {'sch': 0.0, 'source': 'JHU', 'method': 'inferred_zero', 'confidence': 0.6}
      The AI observation disappears without trace.

      Ethiopia is currently shielded from this only by accident: rows 555/556 are
      sub-national, and load_national_rows() filters to Location == "AFR::ETH"
      exactly, so the contradicting evidence never reaches the national series.
      Any country where an AI national positive overlaps a JHU/WHO annual zero
      is exposed today.

  I have NOT changed pipeline code - it affects all 40 countries and is the
  owner's call. See recommendations R1 and R2.

6b. INDEX 41 - AGENT 6'S ANNOTATE-RATHER-THAN-RETRACT CALL IS CORRECT

Index 41 records 50 cases / 2 deaths for 2026-01-01..2026-07-26, quoting WER
No. 35 exactly: "as of 26 July 2026 ... Ethiopia: 1 January to 26 July 2026
Cases 50, Deaths 2". The count and the quote are real, so retracting it would
destroy true information. But WHO's own global cholera dataset on HDX records
Ethiopia's last submitted epi-week as 2026-03-02 with case_total 50 - the
cumulative has not moved since March, and the WER date is the publication's
data-as-of date, not Ethiopia's last reporting week.

Consequence, stated plainly: the gap analyser credits TL..TR as covered, so this
row reports the recent gap as 2026-08-01..2026-09-20 (51 days) when Ethiopia's
true non-reporting tail begins 2026-03-03 and runs about 6.5 months. Agent 6
annotated the row rather than retracting it, which is the right call - the fault
is in how cumulative "as-of" rows are credited, not in the row. Do not read
Ethiopia's 2026 coverage as surveillance. And do not write a zero for it: the
last-28-day columns are blank and ECDC records no Ethiopian submission after
27 July 2026. This is non-reporting, not absence.

    Phantom: True. They currently zero-fill 208 Ethiopian weeks (2011-2014) at
    inferred_zero/0.6 while WHO GHO has no row for those years and AI evidence

    (py/build_weekly_timeseries.py line 374). A higher-priority inferred_zero
