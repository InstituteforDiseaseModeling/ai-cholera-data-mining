   2019 ET-SO 168 is mislabelled; five conflicting 2022 national rows.

   shifted by one ISO week.
D5 The coverage metric counts a camp or woreda row as national-month coverage.
D6 The validator has no cross-layer duplicate, parts-vs-whole, zero-vs-positive or presence-vs-zero
   checks.
