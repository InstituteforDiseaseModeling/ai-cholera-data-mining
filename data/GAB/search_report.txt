5. THE SIX REMAINING GAPS
Agent 6 classified all six as Surveillance_Gap, and no zero row was written into any of them. SG-T means
probable undetected transmission; SG-A means absence is plausible but unverifiable. "Data" gives my
assessment of whether data exist.
 1988-01..02 (60 d), SG-A.
   Ministry dates the onset to 11 Mar 1988. Data: no absence statement exists.
 1988-05..12 (245 d), SG-T to mid-June, then SG-A.
   The epidemic was still running on 24 May. Data: a later count may sit in Ministry or OCEAC paper
   files.
 1991 (365 d), SG-A.
   No cholera return to WHO; the system did detect monkeypox. Data: none was compiled, probably.
 1994-01..02 (59 d), SG-A.
   Devaluation, general strike, riots. Data: unlikely to exist.
 1994-11..1996-02 (486 d), SG-T at low level.
   Confirmed cholera on both sides of the window was never notified. Data: only hospital laboratory
   registers might hold counts.
 1996-11..2000-12 (1522 d), SG-T to 1998 (1997 isolate), SG-A for 1999-2000.
   WHO AFRO records Gabon's cholera reporting as 0% complete or "ND". Data: no national count was
   compiled; the isolate's provenance could justify a single confirmed-case row.

Recent window: no source enumerates countries for any day after 2026-09-20. Checked on 2026-10-03:
Africa CDC (latest issue 2026-09-20), WER 101(39) (data to 30 Aug), ECDC (African data to 13 Sep), the
WHO AFRO weekly bulletin (week 36) and the WHO dashboard (week of 2026-09-14). The window stays open, and
no row was written ahead of a source.
