  Two Level 1 peer-reviewed papers disagree:

        La Dadekotopon 2327 cases / 15 deaths ; Shai Osu Doku  246 cases / 1 death
    PMC5714944 (Nonvignon, household cost analysis, "June to December 2014"):
        La-Dadekotopon 1907 cases / no deaths ; Shai-Osudoku  315 cases / no deaths
