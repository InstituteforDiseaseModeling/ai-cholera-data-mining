THE DECISIVE FINDING OF THIS RUN - HOW TO READ A WHO CHOLERA TABLE

  WHO's multi-country cholera situation reports mark non-reporting countries with a
  section mark and leave their last-28-day cells EMPTY. Countries that reported and
  had no cases carry an explicit 0. In sitrep #29 (data to 27 July 2025):

      Ghana§      2 480   14   7   0.6   [ four blank cells ]
      Kenya         425   20   1   4.7     7   0   0.0   -93
      Rwanda        280    0   2   0.0    13   0   0.0   -41
      § Countries which did not report cholera cases between 1 and 27 July 2025

  Kenya and Rwanda returned zeros for the 28-day window and WHO printed them as 0.
  Ghana returned nothing and WHO printed nothing. The footnote phrase "did not report
  cholera cases" is therefore about the REPORT, not about the CASES. Any agent reading
  a section-marked WHO row as a zero is manufacturing a false negative.

  AFRO's monthly bulletin makes the same distinction even more plainly, in a column
  nobody had looked at: its country table carries a LAST UPDATE date per country.

      June 2025 bulletin, country table:
         5  Mozambique                    ... 29-Jun-25
         6  United Republic of Tanzania   ... 29-Jun-25
         7  Ghana                         ... 27-Apr-25
         8  Nigeria                       ... 29-Jun-25
      June 2025 bulletin, Ghana section, verbatim:
         "In 2025, a cumulative total of 2 325 cases and 13 deaths were reported in
          Ghana, with a CFR of 0.6%. The last report from the country was in April
          with 74 cases, no deaths reported as of 27 April 2025."

  And the May 2025 bulletin, whose Ghana section says "In May, no new cases were
  reported", carries the SAME 27-Apr-25 last-update date. Its "no new cases" is
  therefore "no new report", which is exactly why WHO HQ recorded 6 Ghanaian cases
  for 1-25 May in sitrep #27 while AFRO was calling May a zero. AFRO "no new cases
  were reported" is NOT a nil return for Ghana in 2025. Agent 1 had already retracted
  the May zero built on that phrasing; the June and July zeros rested on the same
  sand.

  THIRD LINE, ARITHMETIC: Ghana's WHO HQ 2025 cumulative ran 2 480 (to 25 May),
  2 480 (to 29 June), 2 480 (to 27 July), 2 554 (to 31 August). The stated increment
  for 27 July - 31 August was 15. So about 59 cases were added retrospectively to the
  period BEFORE 27 July - the months recorded as zeros. WHO backfilled Ghana's missing
  returns, which is only possible if they were missing.

THE SURVEILLANCE-FUNCTIONING TEST, RUN PROPERLY FOR THE FIRST TIME

  The question that decides whether Ghana's post-September-2025 silence is an absence
  or a blackout is whether Ghana was reporting ANYTHING to WHO. It was, and the proof
  is in the same bulletin that carries the cholera events.

    AFRO Weekly Bulletin, week 37 of 2025 (8-14 Sep 2025), ongoing-events table:
      Ghana | Cholera | Grade 3 | notified 31-Aug-24 | start 1-Oct-24 |
        end of reporting period 13-Apr-25 | 7 086 cases | 1 895 confirmed | 51 deaths
      Ghana | Mpox    | Grade 3 | notified 4-Mar-25  | start 21-Feb-25 | ...
    AFRO Weekly Bulletin, week 2 of 2026 (5-11 Jan 2026):
      Ghana cholera event REMOVED from the table.
      Ghana | Mpox | Grade 3 | end of reporting period 28-Dec-25 | 944 confirmed | 7 deaths
    AFRO Weekly Bulletin, week 33 of 2026 (10-16 Aug 2026):
      Ghana | Mpox | Protracted | end of reporting period 29-May-26 | 1 075 confirmed
      "Ghana continues to report mpox transmission across all 16 regions."

  Ghana was laboratory-confirming and reporting mpox from all sixteen regions into the
  same WHO AFRO product that lists cholera events, right through the period in which it
  reported no cholera. That is a country that was looking. It converts the 2026 silence
  from a Surveillance_Gap into a defensible Inferred_Absence, and it is the single
  strongest piece of evidence supporting rows 58 and 59.

  Note also that the Ghana cholera event card was already FROZEN at an end-of-reporting-
  period of 13 April 2025 as late as week 37 of 2025 - five months stale - before AFRO
  finally closed it. AFRO's closure is an affirmative act and supports the absence, but
  it cannot date the end of transmission.

THE FIND THAT REOPENED 1986-1989

  Every WER issue carried a table, "Infected Areas as on {date}", listing every area a
  State Party had notified to WHO as infected with cholera, plague or yellow fever under
  the International Health Regulations and had NOT yet certified free. It is the closest
  thing the pre-2017 era has to a nil-return register, read in reverse. Nobody had looked
  at it for Ghana, because pdftotext -layout scrambles its five-column layout beyond
  recognition; pdftotext -raw preserves the reading order and makes it trivial.

  GHANA'S CHOLERA TRACK, from nine registers read individually:

      29 Aug 1985   ABSENT
      26 Sep 1985   Central, Eastern, Greater Accra (excl. port area), Volta, Western
                    - FIVE WHOLE REGIONS
      05 Dec 1985   the same five regions
      13 Feb 1986   NARROWED to Central Region: Mfantsiman District, Breman Asikuma District
      15 May 1986   the same two districts
      04 Dec 1986   the same two districts
      02 Jul 1987   the same two districts
      18 Feb / 09 Jun / 29 Sep / 24 Nov 1988   the same two districts
      20 Apr 1989 / 14 Dec 1989                the same two districts
      22 Feb 1990   ABSENT
      19 Jul 1990   ABSENT

  (Ghana also appears in most of these registers under YELLOW FEVER - Northern Region,
  Bole and West Gonja districts. That is a different disease and must not be read as
  cholera. The -raw extraction separates them cleanly; the -layout extraction does not.)

WHAT IT MEANS, AND WHAT WAS DONE

  1986 (index 41): RETRACTED. WHO's own IHR register carried two Ghanaian cholera foci
  throughout calendar 1986, so WHO did not regard Ghana as cholera-free that year. And
  the narrowing from five regions to two districts between December 1985 and February
  1986 is itself an act of notification - Ghana was still in active traffic with WHO, so
  the residual listing cannot be waved away as a country that had stopped talking. The
  evidence for the zero was only Ghana's absence from the 1986 annual case table, which
  WHO hedges as "apparently remained free", plus the 1990 sentence "had not reported an
  outbreak since 1986" - whose most natural reading makes 1986 the LAST outbreak year,
  and which now fits the register exactly. Whether cases occurred in 1986 or the listing
  was residual from the 1985 outbreak CANNOT BE DETERMINED, and that is the definition of
  missing data, not of absence. 1986 is left genuinely empty.

  1987, 1988, 1989 (indices 42, 43, 44): KEPT, confidence 0.70 -> 0.60. What saves them
  is a direct WHO assertion the 1986 row never had: WER "Cholera in 1990" says "the
  disease reappeared in Ghana, which had not reported an outbreak since 1986". WHO is
  affirmatively characterising these three years. Against that, a four-year-old unretired
  infected-area listing is weak evidence of continuing transmission - but it is not
  nothing, and it means WHO never certified Ghana cholera-free in this period. 0.60 is
  the inferred-zero cap in py/build_weekly_timeseries.py; the rows now sit at it rather
  than above it.

  This is the second time in this run that a Ghanaian "zero" has turned out to rest on a
  country's absence from someone else's table rather than on anybody saying no cases
  occurred. The first was the June and July 2025 pair.
