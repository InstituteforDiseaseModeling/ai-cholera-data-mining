  "CHOLERA = CHOLERA" carrying a per-country table of newly notified cases and

     /server/api/core/items/{uuid}/bundles -> bundle "TEXT" -> bitstream content

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE INFECTED AREAS REGISTERS YIELDED FOR GHANA, 1970-1990
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The registers give PRESENCE at district resolution and, separately, the weekly
notification tables on the same pages give COUNTS. They are different
instruments and must not be conflated. For Ghana:

  1970-71  Regions progressively added: Accra, Central, Eastern, Volta, Western
           (Nov-Dec 1970), then Ashanti, Brong-Ahafo, Northern (Jan 1971).
  1971-75  Ghana actively USED the removal mechanism. Removal notices naming
           Ghana appear in WER 1971;46(18) (Brong-Ahafo, Northern),
           1971;46(26) (Central, Eastern, Greater Accra, Volta),
           1971;46(31), 1972;47(17) (Volta), 1972;47(20), 1972;47(35)
           (Central, Greater Accra), 1972;47(48) (Ashanti, Brong-Ahafo, Volta),
           1974;49(31) (Eastern), 1974;49(44) (Volta), 1975;50(31) (Eastern).
  1981     1981;56(16) removes Ashanti, Brong-Ahafo, Eastern, Volta, Western.
  1983-84  1983;58(39) removes Ashanti; 1984;59(27) removes Volta.
           *** That 1984 notice is the LAST removal notice naming Ghana. ***
  1984-85  Six regions listed (Ashanti, Brong-Ahafo, Central, Eastern, Volta,
           Western) plus Northern Region with Bole District and West Gonja
           District named individually from 1 November 1984.
  Jul 1986 The Central Region listing NARROWS to two named districts,
           Mfantsiman and Breman Asikuma (Infected Areas as on 10 July 1986).
  1986-89  FROZEN. The same four districts in two regions - Mfantsiman and
           Breman Asikuma (Central), Bole and West Gonja (Northern) - are
           carried month after month, unchanged, through Infected Areas as on
           12 October 1989. No removal notice, no addition, no case figure.
  Jan-Mar  Last entries: Mfantsiman + Bole + West Gonja (25 Jan 1990),
  1990     Bole + West Gonja (22 Feb 1990), Northern Region (22 Mar 1990).
           Ghana then vanishes from the register.
  Nov 1990 Reappears as NEWLY infected: "Accra Region, X Accra District,
           X Greater Accra Districts" (Infected Areas as on 29 November 1990),
           alongside the first Ghanaian case figures since 1985.

ANSWER TO THE 1986-1989 CASE-COUNT QUESTION: NO. There is no Ghanaian cholera
case or death figure anywhere in WER volumes 61, 62, 63 or 64. The numeric
notification table carries Ghana for the last time in WER 1985;60(51-52)
(1.I-30.VI.1985, 25 cases, 0 deaths) and does not carry it again until
WER 1990;65(48) (13.VII-2.XI.1990). The four-year gap in the WER is total.

Why the 1986 zero was NOT restored: Agent 3 retracted it on the strength of the
infected-area listings. Those listings are now fully characterised and they cut
both ways - the July 1986 narrowing shows the register was still being managed
that year, so the two Central Region districts were a deliberate status and not
pure neglect; but the register then froze for three years with no removal
notice, which is the signature of an abandoned entry. Neither reading is
demonstrated, so 1986 is left genuinely empty, exactly as Agent 3 left it, and
the evidence is now in ./data/GHA/cholera_presence_ai.csv where it can inform
gap classification without asserting a count.

THE 2017 FINDING - a WHO nil return that was not a zero
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ghana appears in the WER 93/38 "Cholera, 2017" zero-reporting register, and on
that basis a documented-zero row had been entered for 2017-01-19..2017-12-31
(former index 157, Agent 3). Ghana Health Service's OWN weekly epidemiological
report says otherwise:
    GWER Vol. 2 No. 33, week ending 21 August 2017 : cholera cumulative 15, 0 deaths
    GWER Vol. 2 No. 38, week ending 24 September   : week 37 = 1 case, cumulative 16
    GWER Vol. 2 No. 52, week ending 31 December    : cumulative to week 52 = 16, 0 deaths
                                                     Figure 1 "Cumulative number of
                                                     cases for Weekly notifiable
                                                     diseases 1-52, 2017: Cholera 16"
Only 12 of those 16 fall inside 1-18 January (index 11, Cape Coast Metropolis),
so at least four cholera cases occurred inside the window the retracted row
asserted to be cholera-free. The zero was a NIL RETURN TO WHO, not an absence of
disease. Index 157 was retracted with a tombstone in
./data/GHA/retracted_rows_ai.csv and replaced by three GWER-sourced rows.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE 2018 FINDING - Ghana's two 2018 cases have a place and a date
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GWER Vol. 3 Week 42 carries a full outbreak report: on 17 October 2018 the Ketu
South Municipal Health Directorate (Volta Region, Togo border) was notified of
two critically ill cases among six Ghanaian travellers in transit from Nigeria
to Kumasi; both were laboratory-confirmed and both recovered; 15 contacts traced;
no further case. Those are the two cases the annual row (index 28) carried
without a location. Weeks 1-41 of 2018 carry a zero cumulative and weeks 43-44
stay at 2, so Ghana's entire 2018 cholera experience is now dated to one week.
