*** THE WHO HQ vs WHO AFRO CONFLICT, RESOLVED ***
The two series are not two estimates of the same quantity compiled the same way.
The WHO HQ situation-report figures ARE the WHO Global Cholera and AWD Dashboard
weekly series, and that dashboard is the MOSAIC WHO baseline layer already in
./data/GHA/cholera_data_who.csv. Four independent exact closures establish it:

   dashboard 2025 wk1-4   0 + 572 + 103 + 0      = 675   = sitrep #23 (1-26 Jan)
   dashboard 2025 wk5-8   532 + 64 + 0 + 624     = 1220  = sitrep #24 last-28-d
   dashboard 2025 wk14-17 44 + 18 + 6 + 0        = 68    = sitrep #26 last-28-d
   dashboard 2025 wk18    6                      -> cum 2480 = sitrep #27
   and 675 + 1220 = 1895 = #24 cumulative; 1895 + 508 = 2403 = #25 cumulative.

The AFRO bulletins are a separate regional compilation. They AGREE with HQ:
   2024 year-end   AFRO 4618 @26 Dec   vs dashboard Oct-Dec sum 4616
   March 2025      AFRO 511            vs HQ 508, dashboard wk9-13 511
   April 2025      AFRO 74             vs HQ 68; AFRO's "April" is epi-weeks
                                       14-18, and dashboard wk18 = 6, so
                                       68 + 6 = 74 exactly. Not a disagreement.
They DIVERGE only in January-February 2025:
   January         AFRO 948            vs HQ 675          (+273 AFRO)
   February        AFRO 792 (derived)  vs HQ 1220 (printed) (-428 AFRO)
   cum @ 23 Feb    AFRO 1740           vs HQ 1895          (-155 AFRO)
   cum @ 27 Apr    AFRO 2325           vs HQ 2474          (-149 AFRO)

DECISION: WHO HQ is the PRIMARY national series for Ghana 2024-25, because it
is the same data as the WHO baseline layer the AI layer has to sit beside. AFRO
rows are kept, annotated as the alternative series, and downweighted where they
directly contradict a printed HQ figure (index 63: 0.90 -> 0.50; index 45:
0.95 -> 0.70). Nothing was averaged. Both numbers appear verbatim in the notes
of every affected row.
