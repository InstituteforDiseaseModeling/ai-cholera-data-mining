Gaps still open (260 months = 38% of the series, plus a 5-day tail), with an assessment of whether data exist:
  - 1971-01..1978-06 (90 months) and 1978-11..1985-12 (86 months). Non-notification by policy, not absence. Cholera is
    attested in 1971, 1973, early 1974 and Mar-Apr 1978. Boiro 1999 records that the government of the time
    "avait meme interdit l'emploi du nom de cholera". Guinea reported meningitis, polio and measles to WHO in the same
    years but never cholera (Agent 6). Every WER issue 1971-1985, WHSQ 1971-76, JPRS/FBIS, PlusD cables, Gallica,
    Delpher and the Russian-Guinean literature were searched. Assessment: public counts almost certainly do not exist.
    Any count would sit in MoH internal records. Boiro 2020 Table 2, compiled from MoH report forms, lists no outbreak
    year between 1970 and 1978 or between 1978 and 1986, so large epidemics are unlikely. That table also omits years
    WHO documented (1996, 1999-2001, 2003), so it is not proof of absence. Keep as MISSING, never zero.
  - 1987-01..1993-12 (84 months). Same non-notification pattern. Cholera presence is weakly attested 1987-89, and attested
    in Forest Region refugee areas 1990-91 (MSF-Belgium). Assessment: counts may exist in print. The best routes are the
    MSPAS/SNIS "Annuaire Statistique" 1988-1993 (cited in World Bank SAR 11684; not online), the Boiro 2020 dissertation
    full text (Russian State Library record 01010623893; restricted), Wuillaume 1992 (MSF-B synthesis 1989-91) and the AFRO
    Epidemiological Bulletin 1988 page images (Internet Archive micro_IA41948421_0289).
  - 2026-09-29..present (5 days). No source has a data cut-off after 28 Sep 2026 (ANSS has published nothing since S29,
    uploaded 30 Jul 2026). Needs OEW wk37+, ANSS S30+ or the next ECDC monthly update.

THE DISAPPOINTING NUMBERS
  1. 19 of the 19 months gained this cycle came from Agent 1. Agents 2-6 added 1,222 live rows and 122 sources and
     added ZERO covered months. Their value is resolution (prefecture/commune, weekly), validation and correction.
  2. 45 of the 49 AI-only months are zero assertions (1997, 2010, 2025-Sep 2026). The AI layer supplies case counts to
     exactly 4 months of the 1970-2026 national series (Jul-Oct 1978).
  3. What reaches MOSAIC: py/build_weekly_timeseries.process_country('GIN'), run in memory, gives 2,961 national weeks.
     Only 214 come from the AI layer and only 18 of those are positive (1978). 1978 is the only year whose national
     total changes. Because JHU outranks AI week by week, the AI national corrections never reach the model: 1970
     (MoH 6,378 vs WHO 2,000), 1986 (MoH 9,222 vs WHO's own "incomplete" 286), 1998 (1,232 vs 881), 2002 (MoH 1,361
     vs WHO 61). The same holds for every 2004-2013 checkpoint.
  4. 1,130 weeks of 1971-1993 still enter the weekly series as assumed_zero (sCh=0, confidence 0.5). This contradicts
     Agent 6's evidence-based classification of those years as non-notification periods with cholera present, and
     ignores the 51 presence entries. It is a builder behaviour, not a data defect, but MOSAIC currently sees about 22
     years of false zeros for Guinea.
  5. 44% of all rows (682) cite one document, the UNICEF/Sudre & Bompangue 2009 SAP evaluation. 621 of them are
     prefecture-week values digitised from its Figure 6 (conf 0.45-0.6). The digitisation is faithful: Conakry annual
     sums reproduce Sudre's Tableau V within 1%. The full digitised series, including 41 bars later retracted as
     duplicates of printed figures, matches the MoH totals within 2% in 2006 and 2008 but is 15% (2005) and 12% (2007)
     below them. Use these rows as a weekly shape; they are not complete totals.
  6. Defects in pre-cycle rows: of the 9 pre-cycle rows still live, 3 were mis-cited (rows 3, 4, 5) and 1 quotes text
     that cannot be found in the accessible source (row 1). The rows produced by this cycle passed every spot-check (66
     rows); the only cycle defect was an omitted table line.
  7. The WebSearch quota ran out during Agent 3's batch 2. Agents 3-7 worked only through direct APIs and fetches (WHO
     IRIS, ReliefWeb, Europe PMC, Wayback, Gallica, stat-guinee.org, anss-guinee.org). That gave deep primary-source
     reading but little new discovery of news or grey literature, especially sub-national data for 2014-2026.

  - Digitised chart values (621 rows) are estimates (+/-1-2 cases per bar). 152 minimum-height bars have an expected
    value of 3 (true range 0-5) and carry conf 0.45. Small series run high (Kindia prefecture +3% to +50%). Against the

  - JHU annual rows outrank AI national rows week by week, so most AI national corrections never reach the model.
  - Many absence windows inside epidemic years rest on single surveillance compilations (HDX weekly, MoH SAP). The MoH
    SAP is known to under-notify (Sudre 2009); several regional zeros are therefore "zero registered" rather than proven
    zero transmission.
  - Coverage is a month-level metric: a single annual zero row (1997, 2010) credits 12 months.
  - The 2026 tail (28 Sep onward) is open; the builder labels all of 2026-W40 documented_zero from one covered day.
  - Row 1 remains unverified. The 2017 and 2019 MoH yearbook SAP figures conflict with IDSR, WHO and UNICEF and are
    kept NON-PRIMARY at low confidence.
