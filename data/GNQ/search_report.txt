1. BOTTOM LINE
Equatorial Guinea is a non-reporting country, not a cholera-free one. After six collection agents
(44 batches, 880 queries) and this audit, the data carry usable information for 38.0% of the 682
months since January 1970 (259 months), against 29.9% in the JHU/WHO baseline: the AI layer adds
+8.1 points (+55 months). If the JHU baseline's "phantom" zeros for 2011-2021 are set aside (they
rest on documented non-reporting), only 25.7% of months are evidence-backed (baseline 10.6%).
Counts exist only for Annobon 1973 (deaths, disputed), Bioko 1984-85, the 2004-06 epidemic and a
Malabo prison outbreak in October 2022 (deaths). Cholera is attested without counts in 1973-76
(Annobon, Corisco), Apr-May 1985, 1992-99, 2002-03, late 2005, 2006 and (weakly) 2008. Five gap
periods (12,842 days) remain open.

7. JHU BASELINE CONFLICTS AND MODEL CONSEQUENCES (./data/GNQ/cholera_data_jhu.csv, read-only)
py/build_weekly_timeseries.py ranks rows by source (WHO > JHU > AI) before evidence, and fills every
unsourced week with an assumed zero (conf 0.5). In ./data/GNQ/cholera_weekly_GNQ.csv this means:
 - 2005: JHU UID 894's national 4,400/30 (a nested snapshot, "Primary: False") and the UID 320 annual
   6,391/33 are both used, week by week. 2005 totals 10,237 cases / 60 deaths (+60% / +82%).
 - 2004 (59/1) and 2005 overlap, because WHO counted December 2004 in both years. 43 of the 59 cases
   are spread over Jan-Sep 2004, which AFRO documents as zero.
 - 2006 = 0 for all 52 weeks, although cholera was culture-confirmed to March 2006.
 - 1985 = 108/1 is WHO's incomplete "108*". It is spread at ~2 cases/week over the year instead of
   45/38/25 in three February weeks; the epidemic ran into April-May. 1984 = 404/31 (279 cases never
   dated) is spread from January, although onset was 26 March 1984.
 - JHU phantom zeros 2011-2021 (574 weeks, conf 0.6) override the AI layer's emptying of 2011,
   2013-16 and 2020-21. JHU 2024 = 0 (no evidence label) overrides the WHO-documented AI zero.
 - 1,143 weeks the AI layer left empty as non-reporting become zeros (938 in 1986-2003, 101 in
   2007-08, 104 in 2022-23), including 1992-99 and Oct-Dec 2022, when cholera was present.
 - UID 894's Bioko Norte 400 (to 25 Feb 2005) is impossible against Malabo's 946/15 the same day.
Net effect: none of the AI layer's case rows reaches the national weekly series; only its zero rows
for Jan 2007, 2009-10, 2025 and 2026 do, and its non-reporting classification is turned into zeros.
