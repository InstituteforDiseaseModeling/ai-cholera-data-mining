*** THE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL RESULT OF THIS AGENT'S RUN: A ZERO ROW I WROTE WAS
    WRONG, AND I RETRACTED IT. ***
  In Batch 2 I wrote Index 53 = zero transmission, 2026-01-01..2026-05-03,
  Documented_Absence, confidence 0.90. It was inferred arithmetically: WHO
  Epidemiological Update 38 gives Kenya 26 cases year-to-date at 31 May 2026 AND
  26 cases in the last 28 days, so on its face zero cases before 4 May.
  Batch 5 found that this is false. The Star (Kenya), 2 April 2026, reports
  "Five cases have already been confirmed in Garissa Township subcounty",
  including an 11-year-old from Dekaburey, Iftin ward, onset 22 March 2026,
  laboratory-confirmed 25 March 2026, with no travel history. Corroborated
  independently by Parrot Times (Garissa County Teaching and Referral Hospital
  response) and by the Kenya Ministry of Health, whose own site records a
  national rapid assessment team dispatched by the Health Cabinet Secretary
  handing over WHO-donated cholera supplies in Garissa on 30 MARCH 2026 - six
  weeks before the outbreak the WHO series dates to May.
  Why WHO missed it: the WHO/KNPHI national series dates the 2026 outbreak to
  May 2026 in Dagahaley camp, DADAAB sub-county. The March cluster was in
  GARISSA TOWNSHIP sub-county - a different sub-county - and evidently never
  entered the national notification stream that Update 38 aggregates.
  Action: Index 53 retracted via py/revise_observation.py --delete; tombstone in
  ./data/KEN/retracted_rows_ai.csv. Had it survived,
  py/build_weekly_timeseries.py would have rendered roughly 18 weeks of
  sCh=0 false negatives into the MOSAIC model input.
  GENERALISABLE LESSON for every other country in this pipeline: deriving a zero
  period from "WHO year-to-date == WHO last-28-days" is UNSAFE. It silently
  assumes the national notification stream is complete, which is exactly the
  assumption this project exists because it is false. Do not use that inference
  without a local-language / national-media check over the same window.
