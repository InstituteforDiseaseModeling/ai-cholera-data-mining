from the IRIS PDF:
  "In Africa, 76 713 cases were reported, a 16% reduction from 1992. Sixteen
   countries reported cholera, compared with 20 in 1992. Cholera was not
   reported in 1993 from Algeria, Angola, Kenya, Swaziland, or Uganda, which
   all reported cases in 1992..."

"1990 and 2014" - decade-spanning intervals bracketed by sampled isolate dates,
which do not assert incidence in any one year. Both records left in place and
flagged for Agent 3 / Agent 7.

--- A ZERO I DECIDED NOT TO WRITE ------------------------------------------
The Kenya Red Cross / IFRC simplified Early Action Protocol (MDRKE071sEAP.pdf,
2025) states: "Since 1997 to date, Kenya has had cholera outbreak relief in
only three distinct years when zero cases were reported, that is, 2003, 2011,
and 2014."
That reads like three ready-made Documented_Absence rows. It is not, and I
checked before writing rather than after. The JHU baseline carries WHO Annual
Cholera Report entries for BOTH of the years I would have zeroed:
    KEN 2011-01-01..2011-12-31  74 cases,  2 deaths
    KEN 2014-01-01..2014-12-31  35 cases,  9 deaths
and the JHU baseline also carries a weekly Dadaab camp series running right
through 2011 (e.g. 2011-11-28..2011-12-04, 44 cases, 1 death). So the sEAP's
"zero" refers to the Kenya MoH national line list, which disagrees with Kenya's
own WHO notification for those years. Writing zeros for 2011 and 2014 would
have overwritten real reported cases with false negatives in the weekly
builder. Not written. Recorded here as a documented source conflict for
Agent 5/7. (2003 is unaffected - it is already a zero row in this file from an
independent academic source.)
