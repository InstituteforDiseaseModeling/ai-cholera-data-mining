  The WER 1991 no.40 removal ('Areas removed from the infected area list between 27 September and
  3 October 1991 ... Kenya: Coast Province Kilifi, Kisumu[sic], Kwale, Mombasa; Nyanza Province Siaya')
  is, by the criteria in WER 1991 no.16 ('only official governmental information is used ... deleted
  from the list on receipt of a declaration under Article 7 that the area is free from infection'), an
  affirmative governmental declaration that those five listed districts were free at the time of the
  declaration. It is NOT evidence about Oct-Dec 1991 nationally: (1) it speaks only for the five
  districts and only for the Article-7 interval before the declaration; (2) Kenya's cholera notification
  channel was demonstrably not functioning in 1990-91 (no case numbers in either year's annual or weekly
  tables while these districts were carried as infected), so the absence of a re-listing after 3 Oct
  cannot be read as absence of disease - unlike 1972 (Index 241), when Kenya filed dated weekly
  notifications and an annual total in the same year; (3) the region was in epidemic (Tanzania 5,676
  cases and Uganda 279 in 1991); (4) the next record is the 1992 epidemic (Embu deaths 26-29 Jan 1992,
  Index 982; 680 cases notified 15 Mar-16 Apr 1992, Index 454) and by January 1993 Kenya was again on
  the list as 'Area not specified'. Left as a gap; no row.
