EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
1. Bottom line. Kenya's AI layer is large, mostly well sourced and internally consistent: validator ERROR 0, every
   row reconciles by Index, and in a 60-row stratified re-check the cited figure was found in the source for 56 rows
   (93%), partly found for 4, and found to be wrong in none. But this cycle did NOT close a single historical
   gap, and coverage went DOWN. Three national gaps are still open (1988, 1990-1991, 1993), as they were before the
   cycle. More importantly for the model, most of what the AI layer adds never reaches the MOSAIC weekly input,
   because the shared weekly builder lets JHU's coarse rows outrank it (see point 3).

2. What the AI layer adds (measured in memory with analyze_effective_gaps.analyse(); shared files not rewritten):
   - Coverage JHU+WHO alone 84.3% (575/682 months, 8 gap periods). JHU+WHO+AI 92.8% (633/682, 3 gap periods).
     The AI layer closes 5 of the 8 baseline gaps: 1970 and 1973 (annual zero rows backed by explicit absence
     statements), Apr-Aug 2012 and Dec 2012-Dec 2013 (annual counts plus camp/county rows), and 1 Jun 2025-27 Sep
     2026 (weekly and monthly rows). months_ai_only = 56.
   - Cycle start vs now: coverage 94.4% (644/682) -> 92.8% (633/682); months_ai_only 67 -> 56. The drop is a
     CORRECTION, not lost data: Agent 3 retracted the 1993 national zero (old AI 90) because the WHO register and
     other evidence contradict it, so 1993 is honestly uncovered again (-12 months). Gap periods: 3 -> 3 (recent
     tail 2026-09-01..10-04 closed to 27 Sep 2026; 1993 reopened).
   - Rows 830 -> 2,615. Written this cycle 1,808, retracted 23: 830 + 1,808 - 23 = 2,615 exactly. Of the rows
     present now, 1,370 are case/death rows and 421 are zero rows (380 of them county or sub-county zeros from
     Agent 3's exhaustive-list method). Sources 132 -> 575 (+443: Level 1 112, Level 2 176, Level 3 152, Level 4 3).
     Presence rows 125 -> 198.
   - Resolution: AI national rows now give weekly or monthly detail for about 40 of the years 1971-2026, and county
     or district rows for most outbreak years 1980-2026.

3. The disappointing numbers.
   - Historical gaps: 0 of 3 closed. No public count exists for 1988, 1990-91 or 1993 (below).
   - Weekly-builder reach: I rebuilt the national series in memory with the shared builder (read-only, no files
     written). The AI layer wins only 269 of 2,943 national weeks (9%): 1970, 1973, 2012-13 and Jun 2025 onward.
     Of the 453 national weeks that AI Documented_Absence rows cover, 307 (68%) are overwritten by Fourier-spread
     JHU or WHO counts, which puts 8,374 cases into weeks the AI layer documents as zero. The worst case is 2003
     (MoH: 'No cholera cases reported in 2003'; AI 791): JHU's 1997-2010 aggregate (JHU 611) puts 5,026 cases into it.
   - The same mechanism distorts annual totals against WHO annuals: 2000-2007 = 4,872 per year (WHO/MoH: 0-1,206);
     2014 = 6,293 (WHO 35); 2016 = 10,037 (5,866); 2022 = 7,328 (3,525); 2024 = 6,401 (302).
   - The three gap years (1988, 1990-91, 1993) appear in the weekly file as 208 'assumed_zero' weeks, even though
     cholera is documented present in 1990. These are builder and baseline defects (shared code, read-only
     baseline); agents cannot fix them, and they matter more to the model than anything in the AI rows.
   - Recent tail: Kenya EPR Week 40 (28 Sep-4 Oct) was still unpublished at 11:48 on 6 Oct; the WHO dashboard
     ends at epi-week 24. The national series ends 27 Sep 2026 (AI 887, documented zero). The tail is open.
   - Four rows above 1,000 cases rest on a single source (AI 1679, 2490, 2137, 790). All are at 0.4-0.7 with
     their reasons documented.

4. Gaps still open, and whether the data exist (Agent 6's classification; I checked the evidence):
   - 1988 - SURVEILLANCE/REPORTING GAP (moderate). The health system was reporting other diseases to WHO, but no
     cholera was notified; Tanzanian border districts were infected all year. The only presence evidence left
     is weak: a local account of Kuria 1987-89 (source 517). The Mwaura citation (source 32) does not support its
     claim (I re-checked the citation chain). Any count is probably only in unpublished MoH/DCDC files.
   - 1990-1991 - SURVEILLANCE/REPORTING GAP with cholera CONFIRMED PRESENT Jan-Jul 1990 (high). The IHR register
     listed five districts as infected from Jan/Feb 1990 to 3 Oct 1991 (source 101), and a UN note of 24 Jul 1990
     reports cases. Kenya sent no counts for about 20 months. The 3 Oct 1991 register removal was REFUSED as a zero
     for Oct-Dec 1991 (it covered 5 districts, only the 10 or more days before notification; the register lagged
     events; neighbours were infected). The Bundi 2019 presence rows (source 44) are decade-level phylogeography,
     not dated isolates. No public count is known.
   - 1993 - SURVEILLANCE/REPORTING GAP (moderate). Other diseases were reported; cholera was not. The register's
     'Kenya (area not specified)' (source 494) is a carry-over of the 30 Apr 1992 entry. Only weak presence
     indicators remain (Economic Survey 1994; WFP camps 1993-94). No count found.
   - 2026-09-28 onward - reporting lag; re-check after EPR W40/W41 publication.
