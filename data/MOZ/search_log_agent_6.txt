2. 1978 GAP CONTEXT:
   - Independence 1975; civil war began 1977 (FRELIMO vs RENAMO)

   - 1978 status: Surveillance gap (formal system not yet operational)
   - CLASSIFICATION: Surveillance_Gap

3. 1987-1988 GAP CONTEXT:
   - Active civil war period; health workers targeted by RENAMO rebels
   - CDC MMWR 1989 explicitly confirms "no reported cases in 1988"
   - Mozambican refugee camp in Malawi 1988: 951 cholera cases (outside Mozambique)
   - 1987: Not in SciELO annual data series; likely similar to 1988
   - CLASSIFICATION: 1988 = Confirmed absence. 1987 = Surveillance disruption (civil war)
   - The 951 camp cases in Malawi suggest cholera was circulating among displaced Mozambicans

4. 1995 GAP CONTEXT:
   - Peace agreement October 1992; reconstruction ongoing 1993-1997
   - Health system rebuilding after civil war destruction
   - JHU shows 1994 data and 1996 data but not 1995
   - 1996 had 1183 cases; 1994 had significant cases
   - CLASSIFICATION: Surveillance gap during reconstruction; possible low transmission
   - Note: The JHU database may have simply not had source data for 1995 reporting
