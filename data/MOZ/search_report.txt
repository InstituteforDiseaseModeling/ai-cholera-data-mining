3. THE NUMBERS THAT SHOULD WORRY A USER
   a) MOST OF THE NEW TIME RESOLUTION DOES NOT REACH THE MODEL INPUT. py/build_weekly_timeseries.py ranks
      sources WHO > JHU > AI regardless of method.
        451 of 468 AI national weekly rows (96%) fall in weeks that also hold a JHU or WHO entry, so they cannot
          surface. The same applies to 163 of 476 AI documented-zero weeks.
        1973-2015 in ./data/MOZ/cholera_weekly_MOZ.csv is still ~100% Fourier allocation of JHU annual totals.
        If AI observed and documented-zero weeks outranked JHU Fourier allocations, 502 of 2,960 weekly values
          would change, 46 to 52 a year in 2009-2011. The biggest distortion is 1997: the Fourier spread puts
          ~6,600 cases into Jan-mid-Aug 1997, when the BES monthly table and WHO record none (the epidemic began
          on 13 Aug 1997).
   b) JHU BASELINE ERRORS REACH THE WEEKLY FILE (read-only, reported here, not edited):
        JHU 207 (2003: 13,758 cases, 20,080 deaths) wins over its duplicate JHU 245 (13,758 cases, 102 deaths).
          The 2003 weekly series therefore carries 19,645 deaths (CFR ~140%).
        JHU 62 (national zero 9 Apr-22 Jul 2018) writes 15 inferred-zero weeks, including weeks 21-24, when
          Nacala Porto reported 66 cases.
        Also: JHU 36 (zero 1 Jan-3 Sep 2017, during a >2,100-case epidemic; masked today); JHU 349 (Zambezia
          7,547/78, implausible); JHU 146 (Pemba TL before the first case); JHU 397 (preliminary Jan 2009
          2,655/21 vs MISAU final 4,534/46).
   c) UNEVEN EVIDENCE BEHIND THE ROWS.
        26.9% of rows carry confidence <=0.6; 11.0% carry <0.5.
        970 rows report >1,000 cases, but only ~23% (keyword check) document a second source in their notes. Most are
          successive cumulative snapshots of one official series (DNSP/MISAU), and these overlap heavily:
          30 national snapshots for the 2024-25 wave alone. They are labelled "do not sum", but a consumer
          that treats every row as independent will over-weight those windows.
        Agent 4's 461 rows rest 67% on one Level-2 thesis (Cambula 2005).
   d) ZERO ROWS WERE OVER-LABELLED. Agent 7 applied the orchestrator's rule (Documented requires an explicit
      no-case statement, an explicit zero cell, or a qualified enumeration).
        166 Documented zeros were relabelled Inferred. Most rested on frozen DNSP cumulatives, arithmetic
          closure, omission from lists of affected places, legend symbols or WER/IHR silence.
        Two zeros contradicted by primary counts were retracted, and six zero windows were trimmed or split
          around positive rows.
        The national 2013 zeros (5250/5251) rest only on a frozen WHO cumulative. The cited OCHA "zero cases"
          sentence refers to Niassa up to mid-June 2013. They are now Inferred at 0.5.
   e) LEGACY ROWS FAILED VERIFICATION. 5 of 46 May-2026 rows cite sources that do not contain their figures
      (Index 12, 13, 36, 27, 35). Two were re-cited to the correct DNSP bulletin; three were retracted. By
      contrast, all 226 sampled rows from this cycle's agents matched their sources.
   f) PROCESS. The session's WebSearch budget (200/200) ran out during Agent 3 Batch 9. Agent 3's last three
      batches and all of Agents 4-7 worked through direct fetches only (Bing/Google News/ReliefWeb RSS,
      Europe PMC, Wayback, Arquivo.pt, agent caches). Bing RSS ignores quoted queries.

      calling it with "--help". Only in-flight countries' weekly files changed. MOZ's weekly file has since been
      rebuilt by the runner (18:34) and differs from what the builder would now produce in 52 weeks (1988 zero
      label only).
