SUB-NATIONAL AND SUB-ANNUAL ABSENCE: TESTED, DECLINED, REASONS GIVEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUB-NATIONAL. WHO DON of 26 Aug 2005 gives a genuinely exhaustive early-window
enumeration - "717 cases from early May to 12 August occurring in 4 regions
(Brakna, Guidimakha, Nouakchott, and Traza)". But the epidemic grew to "6
regions" by 21 Sep (regions not named) and to nine wilayas overall (MoH 2011:
"47 localites de 8 autres wilayas", the eight not named). Five of the nine
affected wilayas are unnamed in every source found, so NO specific wilaya can
be shown to have been unaffected. Europe PMC returns zero hits for all six
wilaya names. Creating sub-national zero rows would have required guessing
which wilayas to name. DECLINED.

SUB-ANNUAL. Two well-evidenced sub-annual absences were found and deliberately
NOT committed:
  * 1971-01-01 .. 1971-06-15 - WER "Cholera in 1971" tabulates Mauritania's
    first case as 16.6.1971.
  * 1984-01-01 .. 1984-10-21 - WER "Cholera in 1984": "Mauritania was affected
    by 22 October".
Both are contradicted in coverage terms by JHU baseline rows 155 and 158, which
span those full calendar years with 1139 and 166 cases. An AI zero over the same
days would contradict a read-only baseline row rather than refine it. Recorded
here for a later agent who knows how py/build_weekly_timeseries.py reconciles
layers. DECLINED, not overlooked.

CAVEATS A MODELLER SHOULD CARRY FORWARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHO warned repeatedly that its own notification tables under-capture:
   "there was strong evidence that cholera occurred in several countries which
   failed to declare the disease" (Cholera in 1989); "surveillance and
   notification are incomplete in many areas" (1976 interim review). WHO never
   names the defaulting countries, so this cannot be resolved for Mauritania.
2. Against that, Mauritania was demonstrably an active notifier: it appears by
   name with the correct figures in the WHO annual review for all 17 years WHO
   holds a Mauritanian record. It is NOT an Eritrea-type country that dropped
   out of the reporting system.
3. The 2019-2021 block (Index 45) is the row most likely to be concealing a
   small unnotified outbreak, and is weighted 0.7 accordingly.
4. 1976 (Index 20) is the weakest single year and is weighted 0.85.
5. The 2003 conflict - Mauritanian MoH 445 cases / 35 deaths in Hodh Ech
   Chargui alone versus WHO 34 cases / 8 deaths NATIONALLY - is now confirmed
   from the primary WHO source (WER "Cholera, 2003": "Mauritania - Mauritanie
   34 8") and REMAINS UNRESOLVED. For Agent 5 / Agent 7.
6. Open conflict for Agent 5 / Agent 7: WER "Cholera in 1984" prints
   "Mauritania - Mauritanie 492*" (asterisk = incomplete figures) where WHO GHO
   and the JHU baseline both hold 166 for 1984.
