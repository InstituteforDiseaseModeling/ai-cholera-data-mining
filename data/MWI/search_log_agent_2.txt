THE FIND: A WEEKLY DISTRICT TIME SERIES FOR THE WHOLE 2022-23 EPIDEMIC
  openwashdata 'choleramalawi' v1.0.1 (ETH Zurich, CC BY 4.0, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.13920529) ships a raw
  sheet, data-raw/cholera_epi_weekly_district.csv: weekly cases, deaths, cumulative cases and cumulative
  deaths for all 29 health districts, ISO weeks 9/2022-43/2023. The package does not state its source (its own
  GitHub issue #4). I therefore validated it before writing anything:
   * Summed over districts, the weekly values EQUAL WHO's official Malawi national weekly series in
     ./data/MWI/cholera_data_who.csv for 39 of 42 overlapping 2023 weeks; the other three differ by 4, 4 and
     1 cases (3,325 vs 3,321; 1,435 vs 1,439; 16 vs 17). The sheet's final week (23-29 Oct 2023) is truncated
     (5 vs WHO 22) and was NOT used.
   * District epidemic totals match Nature Communications 15:6291 Table 1 (PHIM database, metadata 98) within
     0-2%; Rumphi 932 and Mzimba N+S 486 are identical, including Rumphi's post-cleaning fall from the real-time
     1,049. Chikwawa (936 vs 748) and Nsanje (690 vs 607) differ because transmission continued after May 2023.
   * Weekly columns sum exactly to the cumulative columns in 28 districts. Defects found: Balaka's cumulative-
     deaths column is not cumulative (weekly deaths used; sum 82 vs Nature 81); Mangochi weekly deaths 120 vs
     cumulative 121; one stray Dowa line without a week (ignored).
  Conclusion: it is the PHIM cleaned line list aggregated by district-week. Registered Level 2, confidence 0.75
  (0.70 on five weeks with CFR >15% and sCh >= 25: Lilongwe wks 46-47 2022, Phalombe wk 42 2022, Zomba wk 52
  2022, Ntcheu wk 6 2023 - validator flags these as CFR_high_flagged_for_review; documented in notes).
