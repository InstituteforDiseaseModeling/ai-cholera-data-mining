METHOD UNLOCK - READ THE CHARTS, NOT JUST THE TEXT
  Humanitarian sitreps put the district table in a bar chart. pdftotext recovers
  the VALUES (they are text) but drops the district axis labels (vector text in a
  rotated group), so the numbers arrive as an unlabelled strip. Rendering the page
  with `pdftoppm -f N -l N -r 170 -png file.pdf out` and reading the PNG recovers
  the axis. This turned a 6-district text mention into a 15-district table. It is
  extraction, not inference; the mapping was then independently corroborated
  against the IFRC DREF list five days later. Recommend this for every country.

TWO NATIONAL SERIES RUNNING IN PARALLEL, AND WHY NO NATIONAL ROW WAS WRITTEN
FOR EPI-WEEKS 43 AND 44 OF 2024
  Those two bulletins report the 2024/25 district figures under a national
  sentence that counts a DIFFERENT series: "Cumulatively, Malawi has registered
  378 cholera cases with 7 deaths (CFR: 1.85%) since 1st November 2023" (wk43)
  and "381 ... with 8 deaths ... since 1st November 2023" (wk44). That is the
  2023/24 season running total, not the 2024/25 outbreak. From epi-week 45
  onwards PHIM switches its national sentence to the 2024/25 outbreak (115, 124,
  190, 298, 306). Writing 378 or 381 against a TL of 2024-08-26 would have
  silently merged two seasons, so only district rows were taken from those two.
