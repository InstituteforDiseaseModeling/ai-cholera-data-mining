1. BOTTOM LINE
==========================================================================================
The AI layer grew from 399 rows / 117 sources to 2,504 live rows / 456 sources. That is 2,113 new live rows
(1,782 case, 324 zero, 7 deaths-only). Separately, 67 rows were retracted with tombstones, 8 of them from
before this cycle. Most of the new data is sub-national. It includes a cell-for-cell verified weekly district
series for the whole 2022-23 epidemic (1,083 rows) and WHO-notification district geography for 1973-74.

The disappointing numbers:
 - Effective coverage FELL, from 99.9% (680 of 681 months, 1 gap) to 92.5% (630 of 681, 4 gaps).
   months_ai_only fell from 147 to 97. This was deliberate. Agent 6 retracted national zero rows for 1979,
   1980, 1982 and 1987: each rested only on silence in WHO's IHR notification channel, a channel shown to have
   missed real Malawi outbreaks (1981: 261 cases reported through the annual questionnaire but never notified).
   In those years neighbouring Tanzania, Mozambique or Zambia were in epidemic. Earlier agents also relabelled
   1975 and 1984-86 from Documented to Inferred absence. A false zero is the worst error this dataset can make;
   these gaps are honest.
 - Weeks of the national weekly series that MOSAIC can use (observed + documented_zero + fourier) fell from
   2,878 to 2,416. AI-sourced weeks fell from 722 to 487. MOSAIC-pkg/R/process_AI_cholera_data.R keeps only
   observed, documented_zero and fourier_* weeks, so Inferred_Absence zeros (inferred_zero) are dropped just
   like assumed_zero. The relabels and retractions therefore turned 417 documented-zero weeks of 1975-1987
   into NA (200 relabelled Inferred, 209 retracted, 8 dropped when row 64 was re-periodised).
 - Almost none of the new sub-annual national evidence reaches MOSAIC. The builder ranks WHO > JHU > AI in
   every ISO week. Of 2,239 weeks covered by an AI national row, 1,752 (78%) are still filled from JHU or WHO.
   Agent 1's sixteen 2022 national periods (17,840 cases, matching WHO's 17,488 within 2%) are inert. The
   builder still shows 32,628 cases for 2022, spread from a dozen overlapping JHU cumulatives, including about
   1,100-1,200 cases a week in February 2022, when one case occurred. It still puts 302 of 1973's 371 cases
   before the first case on 19 Oct 1973, and about 2,454 phantom cases into 2024 (see maintainer actions a-f).
 - Agent 6 added 2 rows from 160 queries. All three web channels were exhausted by the end of the cycle.

==========================================================================================
4. GAPS STILL OPEN, AND WHETHER THE DATA EXIST
==========================================================================================
 - 2026-09-14..30 (PHIM epi-weeks 38-39). Publication lag, not absence. PHIM's media library was re-checked
   on 30 Sep: the newest bulletin is week 37 (Table 1 shows cholera 0, measles 95, completeness 97%). The
   data exist and will appear; no zero was written.
 - 1979-1980, 1982. Uncertain. The routine health information system was working, but neighbours were in
   epidemic and the cholera channel is known to have missed outbreaks. The decisive documents are WHO World
   Health Statistics Annual 1981/1982 (the Malawi blocks) and MoH annual reports. They are probably in print
   archives only; not online.
 - 1986-11..1987-12. Probable gap, during the Mozambican refugee influx. Camp surveillance (MSF/Epicentre;
   Moren et al. 1991) and IFRC 1988-93 appeals would hold counts; not digitized.
 - Jan-Oct 2014. Shown as 42 assumed_zero weeks, which are NA in MOSAIC. Not a coverage gap, because JHU
   season rows cover it. Decision D2: status quo.
 - 2025 Sep-Oct. Cases occurred (26 Sep: 2 confirmed in Lilongwe; week 43: 1; week 45: 2), but PHIM never
   uploaded bulletins for weeks 38-42 and 44, and WHO sitreps conflict. No zero.
 - Sub-annual national counts for 1989/90 (WHO 15,258) and 1991 (8,088) were left unwritten: they rest on
   the WHO notification stream alone, and the only plausible second sources are paper IFRC appeals.

8. MAINTAINER ACTIONS (agents must not fix these themselves)
==========================================================================================
 (a) WHO baseline blanks reported zeros. ./data/MWI/cholera_data_who.csv blanks 48 WHO-reported zeros
     (12 in 2024, 36 in 2025; written by the old converter). Agent 7's in-memory simulation with the zeros
     restored: the 2024 national total drops from 2,949 to 495 (-2,454; week 1 drops from 1,703.7 to 0) and
     2025 drops by 6.
       Fix: regenerate with the fixed py/convert_who_to_workflow.py (delete the file first; the converter
       appends), together with the 2026 one-week date-shift fix.
       Side effect: WHO zeros would then override the AI counts in 2025 weeks 15/43/45 and 2024 week 1.
 (b) JHU 847 year typo. Row 847 (2017-02-12..18, 91 cases / 4 deaths, UID 20823) duplicates JHU 178 and
     1287 (2018-02-12..18, 91 / 4). It renders 2017-W06 as an observed 91-case week between documented zeros.
 (c) JHU UID 3182 (MoH Epidemiology Unit district table) has row errors, broader than earlier logs state:
       - Karonga and Mangochi are identical in every season 2002/03-2014/15 (Mangochi is present, not absent).
       - Lilongwe repeats the same 35 / 87 / 793 / 7 in 2003/04-2005/06 and 2007/08.
       - Lilongwe 2006/07 = 751, above the national 300.
       - Machinga repeats 222 in two seasons.
       - Thyolo is non-zero in all 14 seasons 2000/01-2013/14.
       - Nsanje is blank for 2000/01-2005/06.
 (d) JHU 2022 overlap. Eighteen JHU national rows overlap 2022: 12 cumulative-since-onset rows (two
     identical), row 200, two cumulative-to-date rows, and WHO 17,488 plus four component rows. Together they
     inflate 2022 to 32,628.
 (e) WHO "calendar" totals that are really season or notification windows are spread over the wrong year:
     2001 (2,395 = 28 Oct 2001-19 Jan 2002), 2009 (5,751 = 2008/09), 2015 (693 = 2014/15),
     2016 (1,792 ~ 2015/16), 1976 (19 = 12 Dec 1976-1 Jan 1977).
 (f) Builder precedence. WHO > JHU > AI per week makes AI sub-annual evidence inert in every year with a JHU
     annual row (78% of AI-covered weeks).
       Agent 3's proposal: spread JHU annual totals only inside notified windows when finer rows sum to them
       (1973, 1974, 1976, 1994, 1997), or aggregate complete AI district-weeks to national (1974; 2022-23).
       Also: the builder zero-fills whole ISO weeks whose Monday is <= TR, and it skips the first partial week
       of a non-weekly row (so 1981-W01 becomes assumed_zero).
 (g) WebSearch cap. The session-wide budget of 200 calls (CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_WEB_SEARCHES_PER_SESSION) cannot
     fit six collectors x 240 queries. Agent 4 exhausted it in batch 9; Agents 5-7 ran without WebSearch.
     Raise it in the runner.
 (h) CLAUDE.md contradicts itself on deaths-only rows. "Quantitative Data: Must have specific numbers for
     cases, deaths, or CFR" versus the "MANDATORY DATA INCLUSION REQUIREMENT" paragraph, whose first sentence
     demands sCh/cCh or a documented zero while its last sentence admits "death counts". The writer and the
     validator accept deaths-only rows. Pick one rule.
 (i) MOSAIC drops inferred_zero (process_AI_cholera_data.R). The Documented/Inferred label therefore decides
     whether an absence reaches the model. Confirm that is intended.
 (j) Tool gaps:
       - revise_observation.py cannot change source_index (it forced retract + re-add for row 26).
       - There is no revise verb for cholera_presence_ai.csv (for the CIA and Noticias re-weights).
       - Metadata Source names cannot be corrected: metadata 19 is named "Blantyre district ..." but is WHO
         DON435.
       - same_period_conflict cannot express additive notifications (this caused the 1993 data drop).
 (k) Legacy metadata 1-117 grade 107 of 117 sources as Level 1. Re-grade.
