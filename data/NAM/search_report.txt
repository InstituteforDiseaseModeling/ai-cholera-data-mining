  data.

  (238 Inferred, 208 Documented). The other 2 (Jan-Feb 2025) are documented zeros that a 2025 annual count also spans. No

  1,983 of 2,951 weeks, but 1,968 are zero weeks. It adds 48 cases (+1.0% of case mass), about 45 of them an artefact (O2).

PIPELINE LIMITATIONS (orchestrator and auditor findings; not fixed here; detail in A9)
O1. py/build_weekly_timeseries.py merges by source priority (WHO > JHU > AI), ignoring temporal resolution, and never uses
    sub-national rows. The AI series for 2006-07, 2007, 2008, 2013-14 and the Jan 2018 case never reach the weekly
    series. Examples: 2013 shows 3 cases over 52 weeks, and about a third of 2008's cases land before the documented
    late-February onset.
O2. WHO weeks stored blank but noted "0.0 cases" are skipped. AI 14 is spread into 2026-W6 and W14-17: about 45 cases
    against about 5 really added, so the series totals 322 for the 2025-26 outbreak against 282 (+14%).
    Fix: honour WHO_ZERO_RE as analyze_effective_gaps.py does, except in periods WHO flags as non-reporting.
O3. Week-boundary spill. JHU 2008 puts 155 cases into 29 Dec 2008-4 Jan 2009, and JHU 2014 puts 21.5 cases into the first
    week of 2015, inside a documented absence.
O4. Annual AI rows lose most of their mass (AI 64: 45 cases -> 2.9).
O5. Validator blind spot as above; full-year positive rows inflate months_with_positive_cases.
