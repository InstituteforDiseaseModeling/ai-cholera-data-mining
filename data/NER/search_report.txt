8. Builder caveat (VERIFIED on the runner's 2026-10-03 rebuild; shared code - report, fix outside a parallel run)
 assumed_zero fills 1985 (51 wks), 1986, 1987, 1992, 1993 (52 each) and Dec 1975/Jan 1976 (4+4) with sCh = 0 although they are
 classified non-reporting and 1985 has a presence record. WHO > JHU > AI ranking lets JHU annual Fourier tails override AI evidence:
 1970's 16 cases are spread over Jan-Nov although the AI layer dates onset to Dec 1970; Dec 2008-Jan 2009 receive 4-20 cases/week
 where SNIS bulletins document zero; 2001 = 79 cases (true 194/16); 2017 = 1 (MoH 4). Same-source overlap inflation: 2010 1,506 vs
 1,154; 2011 2,962 vs 2,324; 2012 6,277 vs 5,284; 2013 708 vs 585; 2018 5,244 vs 3,900 (JHU has 236 overlapping national
 weekly-resolution rows in 2018 summing to 25,001). Deaths: 2006 0 of 81, 2014 2 of 80, 2015-16 none. RECOMMENDATION: make
 assumed_zero respect cholera_presence_ai.csv and non-reporting classifications (leave such weeks NA); let a better-evidenced AI
 sub-annual series replace a JHU annual's Fourier allocation; de-duplicate nested same-source JHU rows before taking per-week maxima;
 and keep additive overlapping notification batches (e.g. 2002) additive rather than taking one value per week.
