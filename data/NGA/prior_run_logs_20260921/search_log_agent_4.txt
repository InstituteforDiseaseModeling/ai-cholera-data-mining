A MISTAKE I MADE AND CORRECTED INSIDE THIS BATCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHS Annual 1972 Vol.25 reprints the same 1971 series with periods I-IV identical
and ALL SEVEN DEATHS identical, but with revised CASES for V and VI
(1,361 -> 685 and 4,243 -> 1,338) plus a period VII of 1,370 cases / 909 deaths.
Following CLAUDE.md's "prefer the revised WHO figure" rule I adopted the
revision. That was wrong, and two tests showed it:

  TEST 1 - CFR coherence. Because the deaths series is identical in both
  volumes, the two case series can be compared against a fixed control:
      WHS 1971 cases -> 17.6, 15.6, 15.4, 16.5, 11.5, 17.5 %
      WHS 1972 cases -> 17.6, 15.6, 15.4, 16.5, 22.9, 55.5, 66.4 %
  The 1971 series is high but coherent for an El Tor invasion of a naive
  population and brackets the 12.8% annual CFR. The 1972 series ends in numbers
  that cannot occur in cholera.

  TEST 2 - external corroboration. WER 1973;48(28), published AFTER the
  revision, prints 5,489 for the first five periods. WHS 1971 gives exactly
  that; WHS 1972 gives 4,813.

Both tests point the same way. Rows 1192 and 1193 were REVERTED to the WHS 1971
values at confidence 0.7, and row 1194 (period VII, CFR 66.4%, available from
the discredited column only) was RETRACTED. The deaths value 909 for 1971 period
VII is corroborated in both volumes and is recorded HERE rather than as a row,
because CLAUDE.md prohibits deaths-only rows in cholera_data_ai.csv.
The generalisable lesson: "prefer the later WHO figure" is a tie-breaker for
genuine ties, not a licence to override an arithmetic coherence test.

Also added: 1972 four-week periods I-IV (101/17, 66/8, 31/6, 46/6). These sit in
a different column group from the corrupt one and sum to 244 cases / 37 deaths,
well inside the 1972 annual of 1,363 / 96. All four held at confidence 0.7
because one column of their source is known bad.

A TRAP IN THE WER ARCHIVE THAT WOULD HAVE MISDATED FOUR ROWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WER's issue year is NOT the data year. Issue 1 of volume 49, published 4 January
1974, carries Nigerian notifications for the weeks 22-28 July and 29 July-4
August - meaning JULY 1973. Issue 10, published 8 March 1974, carries 16-22
December 1973. The lag runs up to six months. Anyone extracting these tables must
take the period printed inside the table and ignore the volume year, or they will
shift a year of data. Every row below is dated on the printed period.
