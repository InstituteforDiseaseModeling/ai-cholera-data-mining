through search_log_agent_6 plus the original search_report, with web-search verification
of all major outbreak data points (1991 epidemic, 1999 multi-state, 2010 outbreak, 2022
Borno crisis).

The final dataset passes all validation stages with 99% quality score, zero duplicates,
proper dual-reference indexing, and confidence weights ranging 0.75-0.95 (average 0.87).
