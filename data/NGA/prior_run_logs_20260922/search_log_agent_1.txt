ACCOUNTING CONVENTION USED THROUGHOUT
  A "query" here is one discovery action: either a WebSearch call, or one
  distinct direct probe of a source or archive (a URL family, a feature-service
  query, an archive enumeration, or a specific document retrieved for the first
  time). Extraction work that follows from an action already counted - parsing a
  PDF already downloaded, writing rows - is NOT counted again. Yield is
  (actions that produced at least one COMMITTED row) / 20, in percent.
  Where a batch consisted largely of systematic retrieval from one archive
  already discovered, that is stated explicitly, because a high yield there
  reflects a rich seam rather than productive searching.
