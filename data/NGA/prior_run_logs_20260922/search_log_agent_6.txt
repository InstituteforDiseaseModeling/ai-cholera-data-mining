FINDINGS - this is the highest-information batch of the run.

  (1) NIGERIA'S NOTIFICATION RELATIONSHIP WITH WHO IS UNBROKEN 1974-2025.
      Measles is reported in EVERY year 1974-2025 with no missing year, 52 of
      52. Diphtheria, pertussis and total tetanus likewise run 1974-2025 with
      only scattered blanks. There is NO era in which Nigeria went silent to
      WHO across all notifiable diseases. A blanket "surveillance collapse"
      story for Nigeria is therefore NOT supportable at the national
      notification level, in any decade, including the structural-adjustment
      1980s-90s.

  (2) 1990 IS A TRUE ZERO, AND THIS PROVES IT FROM AN INDEPENDENT AXIS.
      In 1990 Nigeria reported to WHO: measles 115,682; polio 1,873;
      diphtheria 1,768; pertussis 42,929; total tetanus 2,703; neonatal
      tetanus 1,060 - and cholera absent from the GHO series, with WER
      1991;66(42) stating positively that Nigeria "had reported no cases of
      cholera" in 1990. The 1990 measles count (115,682) is the HIGHEST since
      1986 and nearly 3.5x the 1989 figure, so the notification machinery was
      not merely alive in 1990, it was performing better than in the years
      either side. This is a country that was looking and did not find cholera.
      Agent 1's row 55 (Documented_Absence) is LICENSED. Third independent
      corroboration, after Agent 1's WER and Agent 3's GHO-zeros check.

  (3) REGIONAL CONTEXT CONFIRMS 1990 WAS A GENUINE TROUGH, NOT LOCAL SILENCE.
      GHO cholera DEATHS, West/Central Africa:
        1989  NGA 61   NER 22   CMR 2    MLI --   TCD --   BEN --   GHA --
        1990  NGA --   CMR 0    GHA 60   every other neighbour blank
        1991  NGA 7654 TCD 1344 CMR 491  GHA 409  NER 367  BEN 259  TGO 81
      1990 is the quiet year immediately BEFORE the 1991 West African wave
      that killed 7,654 in Nigeria alone. Cameroon files an explicit 0. The
      regional pattern is quiet-then-explosion, and Nigeria's 1990 zero sits
      in the quiet. Had Nigeria been silent while neighbours burned, the zero
      would be a reporting artefact; the opposite is the case.

  (4) THE GHO CHOLERA SERIES STOPS AT 2016 - A WHO-SIDE ARTEFACT, NOT NIGERIAN
      NON-REPORTING. GHO carries Nigerian cholera 1970-2016 and nothing after,
      yet Nigeria reported 45,037 cases in 2018 and 19,730 in 2024 through
      NCDC and the WHO dashboard. Anyone treating GHO as the canonical
      Nigerian series will read 2017-2026 as absence. It is not. Recorded here
      because it is a trap for downstream users.

  (5) 1974's GHO BLANK IS ALSO A WHO-SIDE ARTEFACT. GHO has no 1974 cholera
      value for Nigeria, but reports measles 115,634 and pertussis 31,177 for
      the same year, and WER 1975;50(29) prints 776 cholera cases. Agent 1's
      closure of 1974 is consistent with a functioning 1974 system.

  (6) REPORTING COMPLETENESS WAS VOLATILE EVEN WHILE NEVER ZERO. Measles runs
      182,591 (1984) -> 33,678 (1989) -> 115,682 (1990) -> 12,393 (1995) ->
      217,151 (1999) -> 704 (2006). Swings of 20-300x in a disease whose true
      incidence cannot move that fast. Conclusion: Nigerian notification in
      1974-2006 was CONTINUOUS but of WILDLY VARYING COMPLETENESS. That
      licenses "surveillance operational" for absence purposes but forbids
      treating any single-year national total as a clean incidence estimate.

  (7) NCDC IS DEMONSTRABLY REPORTING IN 2023-2025, so the thinness of our
      2023-2025 sub-national cholera data is a PUBLICATION-FORMAT problem, not
      a surveillance failure: GHO shows Nigerian diphtheria 14,163 (2023),
      10,595 (2024), 15,273 (2025) and measles 20,143 / 14,999 / 21,270 for
      the same years. The machine that counts cases is running. What stopped
      in March 2025 was the dedicated cholera SITREP, a document, not a system.
