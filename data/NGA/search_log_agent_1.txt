DECISION - NO ZERO ROW ADDED FOR 1990 OR 1974.
  Absence from a WHO notification table is non-reporting, not documented absence.
  Nigeria reported 1,078 cases in 1989 and 59,478 in 1991; a true zero in 1990 is
  epidemiologically implausible, and WHO itself hedges ("apparently"). Asserting
  Documented_Absence or Inferred_Absence here would manufacture a false negative
  in the weekly builder. Per CLAUDE.md, Surveillance_Gap is not a valid zero
  label, so these periods are left genuinely empty and recorded here instead.
