ACCOUNTING CONVENTION FOR THIS LOG
  A "discovery action" is one of: a WebSearch; one document retrieval plus
  extraction attempt (ReliefWeb report landing page, PDF, HTML article); or one
  REST/API endpoint probe. A batch is exactly 20 such actions. Yield =
  (actions that produced at least one COMMITTED new row in
  ./data/NGA/cholera_data_ai.csv) / 20. Not sources, not rows, not information.

ANTI-DOUBLE-COUNTING DISCIPLINE APPLIED THROUGHOUT
  Nigeria already carries 10 national_subnational_overlap INFO/WARN flags.
  Every ADM1 row committed here for a period that also carries a national row
  states verbatim in processing_notes:
     "Provincial subset - do not sum with the national row for this period."
  Every ADM2 row nested inside a state row states:
     "District subset of AFR::NGA::{State} - do not sum with the state row for
      this period."
  Every cumulative snapshot additionally states that the figure is CUMULATIVE
  from the outbreak start and must not be summed through time.
