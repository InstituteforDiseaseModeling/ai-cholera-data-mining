This batch produced no rows but four decisive negatives that materially change
what later agents should attempt:

  (1) THERE IS NO WHO ADM1 CHOLERA SERVICE. The task brief hypothesised a
      cholera_adm1_week companion to the cholera_adm0_week feature service that
      Agent 1 mined. It does not exist. Evidence: the WHO AFRO ArcGIS org
      (services.arcgis.com/5T5nSi527N4F7luB) exposes 2,504 services of which 42
      contain "cholera"; a title search for cholera_adm1 across all of ArcGIS
      Online returns 14 items, none of them a Nigeria weekly series; and
      owner:maps_WHO - the account that owns cholera_adm0_week, last modified
      1 Sep 2026 - owns 28 cholera items, every one of which is adm0 or a
      dashboard over adm0. The only WHO Nigeria ADM1 cholera object anywhere is
      Adm1_Nigeria_Cholera, a single static snapshot dated 12 April 2023
      (1,070 cases / 34 deaths over 12 states), which the NCDC 2023 week-52
      table supersedes completely. The WHO global cholera dashboard is
      national-only by design.

  (2) THE NCDC WEEKLY EPIDEMIOLOGICAL REPORT HAS NO STATE TABLE FOR CHOLERA.
      Vol.16 No.32 (24 Aug 2026) was downloaded and read in full. Its cholera
      section is Table 8 (week: 199 suspected, 130 confirmed, 0 deaths, "State:
      6, LGA: 12") and Table 9 (year to date 2025 vs 2026). It gives the COUNT
      of affected states and LGAs but never names them. So the one national
      surveillance product NCDC still publishes in 2026 cannot yield
      sub-national geography, however many issues are mined.

  (3) NIGERIA CHOLERA IS ABSENT FROM THE WHO AFRO WEEKLY BULLETIN. The week 33
      (10-16 Aug 2026) bulletin was retrieved via the IRIS DSpace REST API
      (hdl 10665/387514 -> TEXT bitstream) and read. Its "Ongoing events" table
      carries 18 cholera events - Angola, Burundi, Cameroon, CAR, Chad, Congo,
      DRC, Mozambique, Namibia, Rwanda, South Africa, South Sudan, Zimbabwe -
      and NOT Nigeria, even though Nigeria's ~65,000 cases make it the largest
      cholera event in the region. Nigeria does appear in the same table for
      dengue, diphtheria, Lassa fever, polio and the humanitarian crisis. AFRO
      is simply not tracking the Nigerian cholera event in this product. That
      is worth reporting in its own right and it closes off what would
      otherwise be the obvious route to state-level 2026 numbers.

  (4) A PLAUSIBLE-LOOKING LGA DATASET REJECTED. "NCDC EPIDEMICS DATA_NEMA_
      Cholera 2019 to 2023" (ArcGIS item 1e1ed14daf7f4bf79d22e3f22ba8d678,

      is an .xlsx with five sheets and 748 State/LGA/Year/Cases/Deaths records -

        2021  xlsx 387 cases   vs NCDC 111,062   (287x low)
