 MECHANISM: the "reporting week/month" list enumerates states that reported

 the prior Agent 3 pass rejected, and it would have produced 381 false ADM1
 absence rows here.

 CONSEQUENCE FOR METHOD, stated so it is portable to other countries: for
 NCDC-style products only the CUMULATIVE state table is backfill-corrected and
 therefore admissible for absence work. The timely-reporting enumeration is
 not. This retrospectively validates the prior pass's choice of the cumulative
 flat-tail method.

=== BATCH 4 | NGA — WER "AREAS REMOVED FROM THE INFECTED AREA LIST", 1971-1976
================================================================================
Target gap: pre-1990 sub-national absence, which this file barely has. The idea
is that the WHO Weekly Epidemiological Record printed, every week, a table of
"Areas Removed from the Infected Area List between <d1> and <d2>". Under the
International Health Regulations (1969) an area is removed only when twice the
incubation period has elapsed since the last case died, recovered or was
isolated AND the prescribed surveillance measures have been maintained. That is
a POSITIVE, internationally specified assertion of absence with a surveillance
condition attached - exactly the evidence class this agent exists to find, and
stronger than a weekly nil return. Agent 5 had already downloaded and text-
extracted WER vols 46-51 (507 issues) to /tmp/nga5/v46 ... /tmp/nga5/v51 but
mined only the NOTIFICATION tables from them; the removal tables were untouched.

================================================================================
JUDGEMENT: HOW MUCH OF NIGERIA'S REMAINING UNCERTAINTY IS ABSENCE AND HOW MUCH
IS NON-REPORTING
================================================================================
Overwhelmingly non-reporting, and the ratio is not close.

Nigeria is endemic and has reported cases in every year since 1970 except 1974
and 1990, and in 2025 every one of its 37 states reported. National absence is
therefore essentially a closed question: the 1990 zero stands, and there is no
second national zero to find. What remains is sub-national, and there the
binding constraint is not that the disease was absent but that nobody published
a closable enumeration.

Three separate NCDC enumerations - weekly, monthly and cumulative year-to-date -
were each tested against an independent stream this run and each was falsified.
The failure is always in the same direction: the cholera situation report
enumerates states reporting through the TIMELY channel, while the routine IDSR
weekly stream carries late and backfilled reports that never reach the sitrep
lists. Any agent that treats non-appearance in an NCDC list as a zero will
manufacture false absences at scale - 381 of them in the case I tested.

Where Nigerian absence CAN be established it is almost always because a
sub-national actor published a dense series of its own: the Borno, Yobe and
Adamawa State Ministry of Health daily and weekly sitreps of 2017-2019, which
print per-LGA cumulatives across dozens of successive issues and state absences
in words. That is where the 15 new ADM2 rows come from and it is where any
future gain lies. The 1970s rows come from an entirely different mechanism -
the IHR infected-area list, an international instrument that no longer exists.

Practical consequences for MOSAIC. First, months with no observation in
2016-2026 Nigeria should be read as non-reporting, not zero, unless an
explicit sub-national source says otherwise. Second, the 65 rows of the 1974
and 1974-75 sets are the weakest thing this agent wrote - they rest on
enumeration by exclusion from a notification list that has been shown to miss
isolated cases twice - and they carry confidence 0.70 and 0.60 for exactly that
reason; they should be the first thing dropped if a sensitivity analysis is
unhappy. Third, the single highest-value unfilled request is NCDC's own
state-by-week line list for 2020 and for 2022 H2 onward, which would settle in
one step what eleven batches of inference could not.
