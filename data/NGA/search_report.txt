5. GAPS STILL OPEN - AND WHETHER THE DATA EXIST
 a) 2026 state geography, 27 units without counts. The data EXIST: states report weekly through IDSR. They are UNPUBLISHED:
    NCDC issued no 2026 cholera state tables, and its WER maps give only categories. Route: state MoH/CDC briefings, Hausa
    and national press (needs WebSearch), an NCDC data request, or resumed NCDC sitreps. Classification: publication gap.
 b) National ISO weeks 39-40 2026. These will exist within weeks (publication lag; WHO FS max epi wk 37 for every country).
    Transmission continued (303 cases in the last published week), so never write zeros for them. Re-query the FS, the AFRO
    bulletin and the Africa CDC EIR.
 c) 2014 and 2022 weekly resolution. 2014 ISO wk1-3, 5-9, 50-52 and 2022 wk33, 34, 36-38, 40 have no weekly national row. The
    weekly series exist in non-public datasets (UNICEF Cholera Platform; NCDC). No 2022 WER issue was posted after wk32. The
    2014 wk50-52 holes are assumed zeros in the model input, although same-series arithmetic gives 157 cases / 2 deaths.
 d) 1975-1989 undercount. The national series uses WHO notifications, which are 1.1-25x below federal FOS returns (e.g. 1985
    WHO 30 vs FOS 734; 1987 1,290 vs 2,798). FOS is recorded as a NON-PRIMARY alternative. State tables exist in print (FOS
    Annual Abstracts, state digests), but Google Books snippets drop blank cells. The data exist; page images are needed.
 e) 2000-2013 sub-annual void. National is annual-only, apart from 14 scattered sub-annual rows. For 2000-2010 this is a
    publication gap: IDSR data were printed only as annual aggregates. For 2011-2013 it is an archiving loss: WER-Nigeria
    was weekly on fmh.gov.ng, now dead. Ten Wayback snapshots exist, but web.archive.org refuses connections from this host.
 f) Also open: 2023 weekly (12 weeks, untouched); 2011, 2012 and 2016 state geography (13, 12, 9 states); Bauchi 2019 (9,725
    line-listed, withheld for want of a second source); Oct-Dec 2024 state counts outside BAY/Kebbi.
