HEADLINE, STATED PLAINLY
This run added 2,013 net observations and 210 sources to Nigeria and closed
ZERO additional months of national coverage.  It could not: Nigeria entered the
run at 99.9% national month coverage with only one month outstanding, and it
leaves at 99.9% with that same month outstanding.  What the run bought was
RESOLUTION and CORRECTNESS - sub-national detail, weekly cut dates, and the
retraction of falsified values - not coverage.  Anyone reading the 99.9% figure
as a statement about Nigerian cholera data quality is being misled, and the
reason is in the next paragraph.

THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS MORE THAN 99.9%
Nigeria's national month coverage is 99.9%.  Its STATE-month coverage over
2009-2025, computed on exactly the same ">50% of days observed" rule, is 42.7%.
Restricted to state-months with genuine within-year temporal resolution - that
is, covered by a row shorter than a quarter rather than by an annual cumulative
that credits twelve months at once - it is 15.3%.  (Agent 6 reported 16.2% for
this measure; I reproduced it independently at 15.3%, the difference being
state-name canonicalisation.)  Zero of Nigeria's 37 states are fully classified
across 2009-2025.  The years 2021, 2022 and 2023 show 100% state-month coverage
ONLY because the NCDC full-year state tables give every state a single
1 January - 31 December row; 2009, 2011, 2013, 2014 and 2016 sit between 3% and
7%.  The blanks fall in the same years for every state at once, because they
are the shape of one federal publication history, not 37 independent
surveillance failures.  A report that quotes only the headline is useless.

  1. INDEX REUSE BREAKS THE AUDIT TRAIL.  Four Index values are both
     tombstoned and live: 138, 139, 2601, 2726.  The live data are correct -
     these are entirely different observations - but retracted_rows_ai.csv

     a numeric field, so removing a falsified count costs a retraction, a
     re-add and an Index.

     three URL forms became three sources (Charnley: 58, 359, 413).
  4. The CFR_high check counts ROWS, not distinct facts.  90 warnings, 67
     facts.  Any country mining a cumulative series inflates this mechanically.

     adjudicated, so 20 permanently-unclearable warnings sit in the output.
  6. The validator cannot see ADM1/ADM2 double counts: differing Locations

     national_subnational_overlap only compares national against sub-national.
     Three such pairs existed here; two were one source record entered twice.
