=== DATE EVIDENCE === EWARS epidemiological weeks
EWARS Annual Report 2021 defines the week as 'Sunday to Saturday and starting from the first Sunday of January'. Bulletin issue dates
show the operational numbering is the MMWR-style one (week 1 = the Sun-Sat week containing 1 January when it has >= 4 days of the new
year): 2024 week 26 was issued Saturday 6 July 2024 (under 'first Sunday' numbering week 26 would END that day), week 32 on 18 Aug,
week 39 on 6 Oct, week 49 on 15 Dec 2024 - all 7-9 days after the MMWR week end, the same lag as 2021-2023. So EWARS week N = ISO week N
shifted one day earlier (Sun-Sat vs Mon-Sun); the ISO-week mapping used for AI 168-205, 320-383 and 387-389 is off by one day only.
