1. BOTTOM LINE
The pipeline ran end to end on a non-African country and the validator passes (ERROR 0). The count-type table now types every
source-year 2018-2025 that feeds either series, and no zero weeks were produced. The numbers that matter for modelling are
disappointing, though:
  - Only 3 of the 8 calibration years (2021, 2024, 2025) have a national SUSPECTED count, and each comes from a different
    instrument: 2021 is one WHO annual total with no weekly shape (1,609); 2024 is EWARS sentinel weekly reports of all cholera
    classifications (360); 2025 is mostly a single Madhesh outbreak via the WHO dashboard (1,945).
  - 2022 and 2023 had suspected values before this audit (77 and 14). Both were the wrong type and were removed from the
    suspected series. 77 is EDCD's laboratory-confirmed total by any method; 14 is the EWARS confirmed stream re-dated one week.
  - No national suspected count exists for 29 of the 56 years 1970-2025: 1982-88, 1991, 1999-2003, 2005-09, 2011-15, 2017-20 and
    2022-23. "Coverage" of 87.0% overstates this, because it credits any row: sub-national rows, laboratory isolates, or
    confirmed-only counts.
  - The confirmed series is heterogeneous. EWARS sentinel counts are by definition "clinical and/or laboratory" (RDT included);
    WHO/WER totals are culture/PCR only. The weekly confirmed 2022 series (42) is about 55% of the outbreak line list (76-77).
  - 286 of the 387 AI rows are sub-national and never enter the national series. Nothing is known after mid-July 2026, and
    1982-1988 is still empty.
  - Context: "Cholera is not currently a notifiable disease in Nepal. Although its inclusion among prioritized/notifiable
    diseases has been proposed, it is not yet officially approved." (WHO SEARO, Nepal surveillance assessment 2023, p.52)

Gaps still open, with my judgement on whether the data exist:
  - 1982-1988 (historical). Nepal did not report cholera to WHO, and the press reported "gastroenteritis" epidemics. The only
    evidence is presence in 1982 (Salyan, Jhapa). National counts were probably never compiled; the 1980s Gorkhapatra and MoH
    reports are the only remote chance.
  - 2026-07..11 (recent). The data almost certainly exist (EWARS / new EWARS system, SORMAS), but nothing after week 27 2026 is
    archived and Nepal is not reporting to WHO in 2026. Recoverable later.
  - Suspected-type gaps (years listed in section 1). Mostly the data do not exist: cholera is not notifiable, EWARS reports
    confirmed cases, and the HMIS outpatient "Cholera" column is a clinical label. Possible exception: the DoHS annual reports for
    1999-2006 (outbreak tables), which are blocked from outside Nepal.
