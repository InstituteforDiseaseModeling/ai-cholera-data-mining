                                                        specific non-reporting), leaning PRESENCE
================================================================================
Dates      : whole calendar year 2002.
Reporting  : Rwanda is absent from WER 2003 78(31) Table 1 AND there is no zero-reporting list or
             narrative naming it (metadata 88). Rwanda reported cholera to WHO in 2001 (157) and 2003
             (GHO row), so 2002 is a one-year hole in an otherwise annual series.
Health sys : the national notification system WAS working in 2002: WHO DON 2002_08_23a and 2002_09_04
             carry a Rwandan meningococcal epidemic (506 cases / 72 deaths) notified to WHO. So the
             country could, and did, report an epidemic-prone disease that year.
Weighing   : "reporting meningitis but not cholera" is the argument for a true quiet year. Against it:
             (1) a meningococcal epidemic with 72 deaths is an IHR-type emergency that is notified
             as it happens (it produced two WHO DON notices); small cholera clusters on the Kivu shore did not, and in
             2002 Rwanda had no weekly public cholera bulletin; (2) the annual WER return is a separate
             administrative step from an emergency DON notification, and the years in which Rwanda's
             WER return is missing but RBC bulletins later exist (2013-2015, 2017, 2019, 2020) ALL
             turned out to contain cholera; (3) 2002 was an epidemic year on every border: BDI 577,
             COD 31,658 (much of it in the Kivus, i.e. Bukavu/Goma, directly across from Cyangugu and
             Gisenyi), TZA 11,920, UGA 2,274. Rwanda had reported cholera in every year 1998-2001 and
             2003-2009 (national rows; Cyangugu/Kivu shore 1998 and 2004, Ruhengeri 1999) - the DRC
             importation corridor did not close in 2002. The CDC EID Great Lakes review (presence row, metadata 7) says Rwanda reported cases
             nearly every year 1991-2008 "except 2 years"; 1990 is outside that window, so the two
             missing years inside 1991-2008 are plausibly 2002 and one other (the source does not name
             them, so this cannot be used as absence either).
Verdict    : SURVEILLANCE GAP for cholera specifically: the general system worked, but the cholera
             return did not reach WHO, and the regional epidemic makes true absence unlikely. No zero
             row. No presence row (no source actually documents Rwandan cholera in 2002).
Confidence : MODERATE (0.6) that the gap reflects non-reporting rather than absence; LOW (0.4) that any
