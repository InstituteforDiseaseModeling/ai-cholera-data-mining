7. LIMITATIONS AND CAVEATS FOR MODELLERS (Appendix M quantifies them)
   (a) py/build_weekly_timeseries.py fills weeks that have no source as "assumed_zero" (confidence 0.5).
       Sierra Leone's two NON_REPORTING windows, 1997 W22 to 1998 W1 and 2014 W22-52, will therefore read as 63 zero
       weeks in ./data/SLE/cholera_weekly_SLE.csv. Treat them as MISSING, not zero.
   (b) The builder spreads each JHU annual total evenly across the year and ranks it above AI rows, so documented
       onset dates do not constrain it. In the weekly series, 192 of 1970's 293 cases fall before cholera reached
       Sierra Leone (17 Sep 1970). The same happens with 3,546 of 9,709 in 1994 (before August), 705 of 1,747 in
       2004 (before 2 August), 460 of 863 in 1999 and 8 of 12 in 1977.
   (c) The 2004 year total the builder uses is JHU UID 68's unexplained 1,747/47. The best-sourced outbreak total
       is 633/56 (Index 128, two UN sources; WHO annual 513/42).
   (d) 2015 weeks 1-35 come through as "observed" JHU zeros at confidence 0.9. Agent 6 showed they are
       blank-to-zero conversions from a period with no working IDSR.
   (e) No AI positive count reaches the national weekly series; AI sub-annual detail is visible only in
       cholera_data_ai.csv.
   (f) The modern zero rows (2025-26, 2010, 2018) rest on omission from complete multi-country lists (ECDC,
       Africa CDC, WHO AFRO) plus an operational IDSR, not on a Sierra Leone zero report. WHO's 2025 annual report
       shows Sierra Leone submitted no 2025 data.
   (g) Presence records that were later contradicted cannot be retired with the tools (Appendix K).
