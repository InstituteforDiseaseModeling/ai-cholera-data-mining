6. PIPELINE DEFECTS - MODEL OWNERS MUST ACT (none is fixable in the AI layer)
D1 All 736 rows of ./data/SOM/cholera_data_jhu.csv are coded "EMR::SOM..." (SOM is the only one of the 40
   countries). py/build_weekly_timeseries.py reads only "AFR::SOM" (lines 221, 305), so no JHU row reaches
   ./data/SOM/cholera_weekly_SOM.csv. The gap analysis does read them, which is why coverage looked
   complete. THE FIX NEEDS A DECISION, NOT A PREFIX CHANGE: once JHU is readable it outranks AI week by
   week, and the JHU annuals that other WHO sources contradict would override the AI series:
   - 1998: 4,417 (vs 10,654-14,800 elsewhere);
   - 2001: 2,567 (vs 1,821 in WHO's own WER review);
   - 2009: 208 (which WHO says omits AWD-labelled cases).
   Options: rank AI above JHU in SOM years that have an AI national series, or flag those three JHU rows.
D7 The builder writes sCh = 0 (assumed_zero) for every week without a source row (lines 501-527), so the
   surveillance gaps 1991-01..1994-02 (161 weeks) and 2005-06 (103 weeks) reach the model as "no cholera".
   Mask them as NA in the pipeline. Do NOT fill them with zero rows.
D8 Builder mechanics (do not "fix" the rows):
   - national rows only;
   - rows of <= 7 days go to the ISO week of their TL (Saturday-start 2007 weeks land one week early);
   - an aggregate is dropped when >= 50% of its weeks hold weekly rows, without renormalisation (1985
     loses ~970 cases);
   - overlapping rows of one layer take the larger slice per week, so cumulatives inflate.
D5 (JHU, read-only) UID 21477 rows dated Jan-Mar 2023 are OCHA Flash Update No. 2 of 24 Mar 2024. The
   correctly dated figures are AI rows 9 and 225-228.
D6 (JHU, read-only) UID 20382 has ten false national zeros 2017-09-25..12-03; UID 20375 shows 126...22
   cases for the same weeks.
D9 add-presence (py/add_observation.py) stamps every record "presence_confirmed_count_unknown ... cholera
   confirmed present", even for suspected events, and no tool can edit it. It needs an --evidence option.
(The brief's D2-D4, the 2017 YTD windows, Bay cCh and spellings, were AI-layer defects and are fixed.)
