5. SOMALIA CASE-DEFINITION RULES (apply to every row)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* "AWD/cholera" (the joint suspected-cholera definition Somalia has used in outbreak
  reporting since ~2017, and WHO EMRO used for the 2007, 2011 and 2016-17 epidemics)
  = suspected cholera -> sCh. Laboratory-confirmed V. cholerae counts -> cCh.
* Routine surveillance "AWD" / "acute watery diarrhoea" totals (EWARN, CSR, HMIS
  weekly all-cause watery diarrhoea, often rotavirus-dominated) are NOT cholera.
  They become a row only when the source frames them as a cholera/AWD OUTBREAK
  (outbreak response, CTCs/CTUs, V. cholerae confirmed, "AWD/cholera"). Say which
  in processing_notes. When in doubt, cholera_presence_ai.csv plus a log note.
* Alerts, rumours, stool samples tested, beds, OCV doses, people reached, and
  "people at risk" are not cases.
* Cumulative vs period: state which in processing_notes; a YTD cumulative row's TL is
  the start of the cumulation (usually 1 January), never the start of the week.
