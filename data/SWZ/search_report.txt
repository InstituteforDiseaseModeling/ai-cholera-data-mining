EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
1. Bottom line
 * Coverage rose from 58.4% (398/682 months) at the start of this cycle to 99.1% (676/682) now. JHU+WHO alone
   give 46.2%. Effective gap periods went from 7 to 1, and the remaining one (1997-08-01..1997-12-31) was left open
   on purpose (section 3).
 * Treat the 99.1% with care. Of the 361 months where the AI layer is the only source, 201 (56%) rest only on an
   Inferred zero: Swaziland simply does not appear on a WHO list. Only 87 (24%) carry a positive count and 73 (20%) a
   documented zero. Agent 4 showed that WHO-list zeros missed real hospital-diagnosed cholera in 1984-86 and 1994-95.
 * In the weekly series (py/build_weekly_timeseries.py, recomputed in memory) the AI layer supplies 53% of the weeks
   (1,568 of 2,960) but only 3.3% of the cases (343 of about 10,458). In every year that has a JHU annual total, the
   builder ranks JHU above AI, so the AI layer's weekly and monthly detail never reaches the series. Several of those
   JHU totals are demonstrably wrong (section 6).
 * Validator: ERROR 0 / WARN 0 at both start and end of the audit. However, the validator does not check whether a
   cited source actually supports its row. Checking all 160 starting rows against source text, quote by quote, found
   5 defects that had passed every earlier gate. All 5 are fixed.
 * 2026 tail: covered to 2026-09-20. 2026-09-21..2026-10-02 is uncovered because no source has been published yet,
   so no zero was added.

3. Coverage, gaps closed, gaps still open
 * Baseline gaps (JHU+WHO only), 9 in total: 8 closed, 1 partial (1997).
 * Cycle-start effective gaps, 7 in total: 6 closed, 1 partial (1997). Three of the six closed gaps gained positive
   counts that WHO never received: 1979-80, 1983-91 and 1993-95.
 * Open gap (a): 1997-08-13..1997-12-31, deliberate (Agent 6).
     - Maputo's explosive epidemic began on 13 Aug 1997. The only basis for a zero was WHO non-notification, and
       that same channel missed a laboratory-confirmed Swazi case in June 1998.
     - The CSO 1997 hospital table has no cholera row, which is weak evidence that there were no in-patients.
     - My assessment: a large outbreak is unlikely, but sporadic imported cases cannot be excluded. If evidence
       exists, it is in print only (Swazi press Aug-Dec 1997, MoH border-post records), not online.
     - CAUTION FOR MODELLERS: the builder fills these 20 weeks as assumed_zero at weight 0.5, numerically identical
       to the zero they replaced. Filter on assumed_zero if these weeks should count as missing.
 * Open gap (b): 2026-09-21..2026-10-02 (12 days). Sources will exist once Africa CDC publishes its epi-week-39
   report, ECDC its October monthly update, or WHO AFRO its weekly bulletin for week 37 onward.
 * Weak spots inside covered years (the month rule does not count these as gaps):
     - 1978: JHU 2 cases, unexplained.
     - 1979: 10 hospital-coded cases, weight 0.2.
     - 1987-91: WHO silence only; no hospital tables were ever published for these years.
     - 1993: the 290 admissions cannot be placed within the year.
     - 1998: status after the June isolate is unknown.
     - 1999: JHU's 7 is a stale copy of 1998.
     - 2001 Aug-Dec: JHU 5,612 vs WHO AFRO 2,742.
     - Undated or unplaced small counts: 2005 Nov-Dec, 2006 (18), 2008 (1), 2009 (17 of 19).

 * All six ran at least 3 batches. None reported a yield above 100%. Every gate's row delta matched the agent's
   claim (Appendix G).
 * Most productive by rows: Agent 2 (69 live rows) and Agent 3 (39). Agent 1's WER annual-table sweep and JPRS
   sweep did most of the coverage work.
 * Highest value per row: Agent 4. Its CSO hospital counts overturned 6 WHO-silence zeros (1984-86 and

       agents stopped.
 * I do not judge any agent to have under-performed against the protocol. The binding limit was structural:
   print-only sources (CSO bulletins 1987-2009, MoH reports 1974-95) are out of reach of web search.

6. Baseline (JHU/WHO, read-only) issues that drive the weekly series - for the data maintainers
   Effect = what py/build_weekly_timeseries.py writes into these weeks today.
     2005  JHU 0 cases, yet 64 cases were notified under IHR for Jan-Oct (AI 59) -> 52 zero weeks; 64 cases missing
     2023  WHO dashboard shows 1 case in week 30 (24-30 Jul); AFRO bulletins: last case 18 Apr; WER total = 2
           (AI 177). With JHU's annual 2 also spread over the year, the series carries 4.7 cases vs the true 2.
     1999  JHU 7 is a stale copy of the 1998 figure -> 7 cases spread over 1999 without evidence
     2001  JHU 5,612/107 is about 2.05x the WHO AFRO tally of 2,742/51 (AI 174) -> 5,410 cases in 2001 weeks
     2000  JHU 141/16 all fell in December 2000 (AI 173) -> the template puts 81 cases (58%) in Jan-Apr 2000
     1982  JHU 538/13 is marked "incomplete" by WHO itself; weekly notifications sum to 590/25 -> 52 cases and
           12 deaths short
     1998  JHU 7 omits the confirmed isolate of 20 Jun 1998 (AI 180); all 7 notified cases fell by 14 May
     1978  JHU 2 cases: absent from WHO's own 1978 table, and the first confirmed case was in Dec 1980. Unexplained.
     2003  JHU 32/13 implies a 40.6% CFR; the AFRO weekly curve gives 61 cases and IFRC reports 91/4 (Jan 2003)
   Recommendation: correct or annotate the 2005, 2023 week-30 and 1999 values first. Then let AI national
   sub-annual rows set the within-year shape of JHU annual totals instead of a regional template.
