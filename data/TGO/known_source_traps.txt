TRAP 5 - TOGOLESE ANNUAIRES CONTAIN TWO INCOMPATIBLE CHOLERA STREAMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every edition of the Annuaire des Statistiques Sanitaires / Principaux
Indicateurs de Sante has BOTH:
  (a) the IDSR notification table, "Repartition des cas, des deces et de la
      letalite des maladies a potentiel epidemique par district", sourced to
      "Division de la surveillance integree des urgences et riposte" (or
      "Division de l'Epidemiologie"); and
  (b) routine HMIS "Cas de consultation" and "Causes d'hospitalisation" tables
      by region and age/sex, which also carry a "Cholera" line.
In PIS 2016 these read, for the SAME year:
  (a) Ensemble pays = 2 cases, 0 deaths
  (b) Lome Commune 279 + Plateaux 194 + Kara 117 + Savanes 35 + Centrale 24 +
      Maritime 5 = 654 consultations, plus hospitalisation lines (Lome 7,
      Savanes 17).
654 against 2, in one document, for one year. Stream (b) is a diagnostic code
in the consultation register dominated by acute watery diarrhoea. An agent that
greps a Togolese annuaire for "cholera" hits stream (b) FIRST.
RULE: in any Togolese annuaire, extract ONLY from the table whose source line
names the Division de la surveillance integree / Division de l'Epidemiologie.
Never from "Cas de consultation" or "Causes d'hospitalisation".

*** THE DECISIVE TEST FOR ANY WHO-SOURCED TOGOLESE ZERO ***
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do NOT ask "is Togo absent from the WER cholera table". Ask "IS TOGO IN THE
ZERO-REPORTING LIST". WHO's cholera annuals carry an explicit list of the
countries that "reported 0 cases and deaths ... fulfilling the vital public
health surveillance service of 'zero reporting'". Being IN it is a printed
nil return. Being merely absent from the case table is not a zero - WHO's
own footnote says so, and Togo proves it.
RESULT OF RUNNING THAT TEST ON EVERY WER CHOLERA ANNUAL 2013-2024:
  Togo IN the zero-reporting list  : 2018, 2019, 2022  (and only these)
  Togo REPORTED CASES              : 2013, 2014, 2015, 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024
  Togo ABSENT FROM BOTH            : *** 2016 AND 2017 ***
2016 and 2017 are TWO CONSECUTIVE FAILED ANNUAL RETURNS. The string "Togo"
occurs ZERO times in WER 2018;93(38) "Cholera, 2017". That is why WER
Cholera-2020's "Togo reported its first cases since 2015" exists - WHO had no
2016 or 2017 Togolese return to contradict it with - and it is why that
sentence is WRONG for 2016, where Togo's own Annuaire records 2 cases.
The 1980s CANNOT pass this test at all: WER "Cholera in 1985", "1987" and
"1989" were read in full and contain NO zero-reporting instrument. The
footnote is a later WHO innovation. So no 1980s Togolese zero can ever be
Documented_Absence; Inferred_Absence is the ceiling the evidence allows.
