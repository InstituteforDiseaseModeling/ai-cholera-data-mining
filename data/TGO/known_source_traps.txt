TRAP 1 — 1997 Dapaong outbreak is MENINGITIS, not cholera
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: WHO Disease Outbreak News, 27 January 1997.
Reports 739 cases / 121 deaths in Dapaong, Savanes region, Togo.
THE DISEASE IS MENINGITIS (epidemic meningococcal meningitis, the 1997
Sahelian belt season). It is NOT cholera.

Why it keeps being ingested: search engines and AI result summaries render
this DON as a cholera event when it is retrieved by a cholera-scoped query.
At least three independent agents across two concurrent runs (Agent 2 and
Agent 3 lineages) were served it as an apparent cholera hit.

Action: never create a cholera row from this DON at any geography.
The 121 deaths / 739 cases figures must not appear in
./data/TGO/cholera_data_ai.csv or ./data/TGO/cholera_presence_ai.csv.

TRAP 2 — WHO multi-country cholera update moved into the WER (July 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
From July 2026 the WHO multi-country cholera external situation report is
published INSIDE the Weekly Epidemiological Record (e.g. WER 101(31),
WER 101(35)). The former path who.int/publications/m/item/... is DEAD.

back to ECDC's Communicable Disease Threats Report and attributed it as
though it were WHO. ECDC is a separate source with its own reliability
level and must be cited as ECDC.

Status as checked 2026-09-19: Togo is ABSENT from both WER 101(31) and
WER 101(35).

OPEN REPAIR — row Index 25 is mislabelled Documented_Absence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Raised by Agent 3, 2026-09-19. Row 25 (AFR::TGO, 2026-01-01..2026-03-29,
sCh=0) carries evidence label Documented_Absence but rests on NON-LISTING
evidence, i.e. "Togo does not appear in this table" rather than "Togo
reported zero". It should be Inferred_Absence. Three findings support the
downgrade:
  1. WHO's own footnote: "Missing data in this report do not imply the
     absence of cholera."
  2. ECDC CDTR week 9 2026 lists Togo under "no updates have been reported
     by ..." — a list that ALSO contains South Sudan, Chad, Congo and
     Ethiopia while those countries were actively reporting cases. Absence
     from the table therefore demonstrably does not mean absence of disease.
  3. WHO's GHO series for Togo contains no zeros at all, and omits 1990 —
     a year with 136 real cases. Non-appearance in GHO is not a zero.
Agent 3 could not apply the fix: py/add_observation.py has no edit
subcommand. The caveat is registered as metadata source 219 so it travels
with the data. ACTION FOR AGENT 7 / a future repair pass: relabel Index 25
to Inferred_Absence and consider lowering its confidence_weight.
