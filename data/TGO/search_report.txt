2. THE THREE THINGS THAT MATTERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(a) A FALSE NATIONAL ZERO WAS DESTROYED, AND IT WAS HIDING A YEAR OF DISEASE.
    AI row Index 25 asserted sCh=0 / deaths=0 for 2026-01-01..2026-03-29 at
    Documented_Absence, confidence 0.95, on the sole evidence that Togo does not
    appear in WHO multi-country cholera epidemiological update #36 - a report
    whose own footnote reads "Missing data in this report do not imply the
    absence of cholera". Togo's own return refutes it: BNTIS No 01/T1/2026,
    Tableau XIX (source DHIS2) prints cumulative weeks 1-13 of 2026 = 12
    suspected cases / 1 death. Row 25 is RETRACTED with a tombstone and replaced
    by AI row 344.
    CONSEQUENCE: latest_positive_observation moved 2025-03-23 -> 2026-03-29.
    Nearly a year of apparent Togolese quiescence was an artefact of one
    mislabelled row. The rebuilt weekly series moves calendar 2026 from 0.0
    cases across 35 AI-asserted zero weeks to 12.0. This is the single most
    consequential correction of the run.

(b) A SECOND ROW OF THE SAME CLASS SURVIVED FIVE AGENTS, AND THE CHECKER CANNOT
    SEE IT. AI row Index 108 asserted a national zero for 2024-01-01..2024-06-30
    at Documented_Absence 0.90 while AI row 334 - also from Togo's own BNTIS,
    also DHIS2 - records 1 suspected case in ISO weeks 1-13 of 2024. Narrowed to
    2024-04-01, relabelled Inferred_Absence, 0.90 -> 0.55.
    CHECKER LIMITATION, FLAGGED FOR REPAIR: py/validate_quality.py's
    same_period_conflict fires only on IDENTICAL location AND period. A positive
    row NESTED INSIDE a longer zero row is invisible to it. That is how row 108
    survived. This is the SECOND row of this class found on one country, so it is
    not a fluke. The sweep that does catch it - every zero row x every positive
    row, testing location containment x period overlap, ranked by overlap divided
    by the POSITIVE row's own length - was re-run by this audit and returned 55
    dangerous-direction overlaps on TGO, of which zero remain unexplained. It
    should be added to py/validate_quality.py and run on all 40 countries.

(c) TWO NATIONAL SERIES WERE ADJUDICATED, AND NEITHER ADJUDICATION REACHES THE
    MODEL. 2001: the baseline 2,551 was shown to be a truncated notification sum
    stopping 14 October; WHO's own published annual review (WER 2002;77(31)
    Table 1) gives 2,696/132 (AI row 342), and the Ministry's 2,740 is then only
    1.6% above WHO's own number rather than 7.4%. 2011: determined at 33 cases /
    1 death against a notified annual of 4, on six Togolese instruments including
    the only contemporaneous one (BETTOGO n6, December 2011).
    THE DISAPPOINTING PART: py/build_weekly_timeseries.py ranks sources
    {WHO:3, JHU:2, AI:1}, so the weekly product still renders 2001 as 2,446 and
    2011 as 4.0 - both values this run proved to be under-counts. The
    adjudications are documentation for humans and are invisible to the model.

weekly total against the highest value any source claims. Two years exceed every

  2024: weekly series sums to 432.9 against a documented annual of 281 (+54%).
        CAUSE: AI rows 202-206 are the WHO AFRO monthly increments (they sum to
        exactly 281) while the WHO BASELINE layer separately carries the same
        epidemic as a weekly stream summing to 282. The builder computes its
        "already covered by observed weekly data" guard PER SOURCE LAYER, so
        when it processes AI it cannot yet see WHO's weekly rows; the monthlies
        Fourier-spread in full and survive in the six weeks WHO does not cover
        (40, 41, 43, 46, 47, 48), adding 122.9 cases on top.

        and against WHO's 164 (+37%). THIS RUN CAUSED IT: the series read 166.1
        before. AI rows 345 (183 at week 13) and 348 (209 at week 25) are NESTED
        READINGS OF ONE CUMULATIVE Togolese DSIUR/DHIS2 SERIES; the builder
        treats them as two independent period totals and takes a per-week
        maximum instead of differencing them (209 - 183 = 26 cases over weeks
        14-25).
        NET VERDICT ON THIS CHANGE: it improved the documentary record - the 209
        is now a row instead of prose buried in other rows' notes - and degraded

NO ROW WAS RETRACTED TO FIX ANY OF THIS. Every row involved is a faithful source

9. REUSABLE METHODS DISCOVERED THIS RUN (these generalise beyond Togo)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. WHEN A GOVERNMENT SITE SERVES AN EMPTY SPA SHELL, READ THE RESPONSE'S
    Content-Security-Policy connect-src HEADER. Three runs and six agents
    recorded sante.gouv.tg as dead because it serves an identical ~17.6 KB React
    shell for every path. The CSP header names the back-end the SPA fetches
    from: bk.sante.gouv.tg, a live WordPress instance with an open REST API that
    both LISTS and SERVES the PDFs -
      /wp-json/wp/v2/media?search=<term>&per_page=100
      /wp-json/wp/v2/documentation?per_page=100
    It yielded the Rapport annuel de performance 2024 (the top unretrieved
    document of the previous run, proven absent from Common Crawl), BNTIS
    T1/2026 and the PNDS mid-term evaluation. Full inventory taken: 113 PDFs.
 2. COMMON CRAWL WARC BYTE-RANGE REQUESTS RECOVER PRE-BREAKAGE PDFs FROM ROTTED
    GOVERNMENT DOMAINS, and work when the Internet Archive is down.
    index.commoncrawl.org/collinfo.json -> {CRAWL}-index?url=...&output=json ->
    HTTP Range request against data.commoncrawl.org/{filename} using the offset
    and length fields. It holds 79 real PDF captures of sante.gouv.tg taken
    before that site broke. Practical budget ~100 index queries per session.
 3. files.aho.afro.who.int HAS AN EXPIRED TLS CERTIFICATE. WebFetch refuses and
    plain curl returns nothing; `curl -k` works. Its filenames are
    country-agnostic - always read the title page.
 4. USE A PARALLEL NOTIFIABLE-DISEASE CHANNEL AS A REPORTING HEARTBEAT. Under
    IHR(1969) exactly three diseases were notifiable: cholera, plague and yellow
    fever. A Togo entry in a WER yellow-fever notification table therefore proves
    the SAME channel that would have carried cholera was open. WER "Yellow fever
    in 1984" records Togo notifying a fatal, twice-laboratory-confirmed case in
    November 1984 with a WHO-supported 300,000-person vaccination response -
    inside the cholera-free run, in one of its two demanding years. Cheaper and
    more complete still: the WHO GHO notifiable-disease series
    (ghoapi.azureedge.net/api/WHS3_{41,43,46,...}?$filter=SpatialDim eq 'TGO')
    shows Togo filed a return in EVERY year 1974-2025.
