CRITICAL FINDING THAT RE-AIMS THIS AGENT'S TARGETS
--------------------------------------------------
The launch brief asked me to prioritise district breakdowns for 2007-2011 and 2012
because ./data/UGA/cholera_data_ai.csv holds those years only as national aggregates
(row 33 = 7,615 cases 2007-2011; row 35 = 6,226 cases 2012). That is true of the AI
layer in isolation, but it is NOT true of the merged dataset. The JHU baseline file
./data/UGA/cholera_data_jhu.csv already carries district-level counts for exactly
those years, and they very nearly reconcile with the national aggregates:

                 Zombo 34, Maracha 30, Manafwa 25, Sironko 25, Butaleja 17, Masindi 7,
                 Koboko 2, Nwoya 2   vs AI row 35 national 6,226 (JHU sums higher; the
                 JHU rows are IDSR weeklies that partly double-count cumulative updates)
    2013  1,599 | 2014  322 | 2015 15,805 | 2016 3,609 | 2017 1,569 | 2018 21,364 | 2019 241
    Karamoja 2010 is fully district-resolved in JHU (Moroto/Kotido/Amudat/Nakapiripirit).

Re-searching 2007-2012 district breakdowns would therefore spend the query budget
re-deriving numbers the merged dataset already has, and would risk creating AI rows
that duplicate JHU district rows across layers. Per CLAUDE.md the effective gap is
what remains after JHU + WHO + AI, so I re-aimed at the district-periods that are
genuinely unresolved in ALL THREE layers:

  P1  2020  national 1,361-1,521 cases; only Moroto (458, AI row 4) is district-resolved.
            ~900-1,000 cases have no geography anywhere. ZERO JHU sub-national rows for 2020.
  P2  2025  Kikuube/Kyangwali and Kiryandongo named in AI row 13 notes but never given
            their own rows; Lamwo/Adjumani/Moyo present.
  P3  2023-2024 thin: JHU has Namayingo 68 + Kayunga 56 (2023), Masaka 84 (2024);
            AI has Kayunga 78, Namayingo 24, Kyotera 64, Elegu 13.
  P4  1997-1998 the largest epidemic in Uganda's history (~50,000 cases, AI rows 2-3)
            has NO district detail in any layer. JHU has no sub-national row before 2002.
  P5  1971-1996 no district detail in any layer for any year.
  P6  2021-2022 2021 accounted for by Nakivale; WHO annual reports 0 for 2022.
  P7  Karamoja outside 2010; Lake Victoria islands; West Nile refugee districts.
