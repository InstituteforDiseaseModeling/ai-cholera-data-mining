*** THE CENTRAL FINDING OF THIS AGENT'S RUN - HOW "227" WAS MANUFACTURED ***
The WHO AFRO weekly monitored-events table columns are:
  Country | Event | Grade | Date notified to WCO | Start of reporting period |
  End of reporting period | Total cases | Cases Confirmed | Deaths | CFR
Uganda's cholera row across the June-August 2025 issues:
  wk27 (30 Jun-6 Jul)  notified -        start 26-Jun-25  END 26-Jun-25   26 / 3  / -  / 0.0%
       narrative: "outbreak is ongoing in Adjumani (21) and Moyo (5) districts... Three
       confirmed cases were reported out of five suspected cases. No death has been reported"
  wk28 (7-13 Jul)      start 26-Jun-25   END 02-Jul-25   120 / 37 / 2  / 1.7%
       narrative: "ongoing in Uganda with 37 cases registered and two deaths"
  wk29 (14-20 Jul)     start 26-Jun-25   END 02-Jul-25   120 / 37 / 2  / 1.7%   (identical)
  wk30 (21-27 Jul)     start 12-Jun-25   END 02-Jul-25   227 / 13 / 2  / 0.90%
       narrative: "ongoing in Uganda with 13 cases registered and two deaths"
  wk31-34: 227 / 13 / 2 / 0.90% repeated verbatim, then dropped from the table at wk35.
The total went 26 -> 120 -> 120 -> 227 while the END OF REPORTING PERIOD never moved off
2 July 2025 and the narrative count FELL from 37 to 13. A cumulative that rises with no
advance in the reporting cut-off, alongside a falling narrative, is a data-entry regression,
not transmission. The CFR cell is derived from whatever the totals cell says (2/120 = 1.7%,
2/227 = 0.9%), so it corroborates nothing.
GROUND TRUTH from Uganda MoH / UNIPH field investigations (line lists, culture confirmation,
unmatched case-control):
  Adjumani "We identified 19 cases (4 confirmed and 15 probable)... case fatality rate of
            4.3% (1/19)", case-search window 1 May - 10 Jul 2025 (row 73)
  Moyo     "We identified 17 confirmed cases with no deaths", 5 Jun - 8 Jul 2025 (row 74)
  TOTAL    36 cases, 1 death.
AFRO monthly June 2025: "As of 29 June 2025, Uganda's cumulative cases and deaths were 451
and three deaths... In June, 248 new cases were reported with no deaths. There were no cases
reported in May." 451 exceeds AFRO's own weekly tracker (227) and is ~12x the field count.
WHO HQ sitrep 29 (1 Jan-27 Jul 2025): Uganda 255 cases / 0 deaths, last-28-days 99 / 0.
WHO HQ epi update 33 (27 Jan 2026): Uganda 255 / 0 for the whole of 2025 - the series froze
in July 2025 and never moved.
ARITHMETIC THAT DECIDES IT: AFRO's own 23 March cumulative is 203. 203 + 52 = 255 (the WHO HQ
annual). 203 + 248 = 451 (the AFRO monthly). A June-July episode of 36-52 cases is compatible
with 255; one of 248 is not compatible with anything Uganda's investigators found.
