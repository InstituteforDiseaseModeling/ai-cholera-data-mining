1. SUMMARY - THE DISAPPOINTING NUMBERS FIRST
- Coverage is LOWER than at the start of the run: 85.2% -> 84.2% (581 -> 574 of 682 months).
  This is deliberate. Agent 1 retracted national zero row 58 (Mar-Dec 1983). The zero had been
  inferred from Uganda's absence from a WHO table, and it is contradicted by a Busia count in
  April 1983 and by Lake Albert deaths in January 1984. Removing it reopened May-Dec 1983
  (-8 months). Row 1012 then closed September 2026 (+1).
- Five historical gaps (107 months) remain open: 1981; Jan-Mar 1983; May 1983-Dec 1986;
  1988-1990; 1993. About 1,200 queries this cycle found no case count for any of those months.
  The only finds were two deaths-only items (Masindi, Jan 1984; Kampala, Dec 1986-Feb 1987) and
  some count-less presence records. All five are SURVEILLANCE GAPS and none was zero-filled.
- Four agents hit the 12-batch ceiling (1, 2, 3, 5). For three of them (1, 2, 5) the stopping
  criterion never triggered: yield was still on the table (Agent 1's last batch 30%, Agent 2's 40%).
- Agent 4 found nothing: 120 queries, 0 rows, after a one-time redeployment. This was a genuine
  negative after an exhaustive archival search.
- Agents 4-6 had NO WebSearch. The session cap of 200 calls ran out in Agent 3's batch 11.
- The host slept twice during Agent 2, leaving two unscored partial batches. Their rows were
  verified and kept.
- The spot audit (46 rows) found 1 row of wrong data: row 357, a 1971 SMALLPOX notification
  recorded as cholera. 3 more rows needed a citation or date correction.

  2023-2025. It also asserts about 468 zero weeks inside the gap years. These are builder and
  baseline defects (A11).
