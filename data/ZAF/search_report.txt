5. ESCALATIONS FOR THE USER (baseline files and builder; agents cannot change either)
B0 Template artefact. The ZAF Fourier template (country_k1) is fitted on 8 weekly baseline rows, dominated by the WHO
   2023-08-28 cumulative lump and one JHU 2004 week. It peaks in ISO week 22 (late May) and troughs in week 48, at 4:1;
   SA cholera peaks in summer. All 1,762 Fourier-spread weeks inherit this shape. Example: 1980 places 697 of 859 cases in
   Jan-Aug, before the September 1980 onset.
B1 2005: JHU 26 (WHO 3,503 cases / 28 deaths) is spread over the whole year. AI 974 (NDoH monthly 0/0, NICD 0, WER 0) is a
   Documented zero but loses because the builder ranks JHU above AI. Agent 6 classifies the WHO figure as a data error
   (high confidence). Result: +3,503 false-positive cases.
B2 2008: builder 6,829 cases / 44 deaths vs WHO 3,907/22. It places 3,807 cases in Jan-Oct (JHU 28 annual + JHU 60 Aug-Dec),
   although the outbreak began on 15 Nov 2008 (Jan-Oct had ~34 laboratory-confirmed sporadic cases). Nov-Dec gets 3,022
   cases vs NOC 1,320/13 at 27 Dec.
B3 2009: builder 12,896/72 vs WHO 10,520/57 (JHU 56 nested inside JHU 29). 5,513 cases land in Jun-Dec, after the season
   ended.
B4 2023: builder 3,044/104 vs ~1,390-1,478/47. Nested JHU cumulative snapshots (JHU 145/146/65) fill Jan-Aug, then WHO
   Index 1 adds the cumulative 1,390/47 in the week 2023-08-28..09-03. Jan 2023 gets 77 cases before the 1 Feb onset.
   The AI onset-week series (AI 2215-2250: 1,299 cases, 55 deaths, peak 342 in the week of 22 May) is never used. For
   27 Nov-24 Dec 2023, WHO's weeks show 1 case where the AI onset rows record 31.
B5 2000 and 2002: 2000 = 28,349 vs WHO 19,667, with 14,517 cases placed in Jan-Jul before the mid-August onset. 2002 =
   56,221 vs WHO 10,004 or NDoH 16,394 (JHU 72 Jan-May 28,000 + JHU 57's 2000-02 aggregate, 27,175 in Jun-Dec).
B6 2004: 4,605 vs WHO 2,767; the JHU annual row and four JHU sub-period rows are both used.
B7 Years where AI is the top layer: a same-source aggregate beats the precise row. 1988 renders 67 vs 5 (AI 1956); 1989-90
   135 vs ~9-18; 1995 24 vs 2 (AI 1967); 1997 now 16.9 vs 1, because AI 667 outranks inferred zeros. AI 657 and AI 667 are
   NOT rendered as zeros (requirement met). 2007 and 2010 render correctly (zeros plus the June 2010 import), and after
   WHO's last week (2026-07-19) the series carries AI documented zeros to 2026-09-30.
B8 JHU annual zeros override AI national case rows: 2011 (AI 505, 1 confirmed), 2014 (AI 472, 2), 2020 (AI 530, 1). They
   also downgrade AI documented zeros to inferred_zero in 2006, 2011-12, 2014-17 and 2019-21.
B9 WHO zero weeks override AI cases. All 52 WHO weeks of 2025 are 0, yet WHO's own 2025 annual is 4 (AI 66) and NMCSS
   notified 2/2/5/24 cases in Mar/Apr/Jun/Jul, plus 4 suspected in Dec. MOSAIC sees 0 for 2025. In 2024 the WHO weeks are
   confirmed-only (11 cases, 0 deaths) vs NICD 162 notified / 13 confirmed / 5 deaths.
B10 Zero rows ending or starting mid-week claim the whole ISO week, so the 7 Feb and 19 Nov 2018 cases vanish (neither has
   a national row either). WHO 2026 weeks match AI onsets (8 and 15 Jul); no week shift is visible for ZAF.
Net: +67,191 cases in 2000, 2002, 2004, 2005, 2008, 2009 and 2023 against WHO's own annual totals. Suggested fixes: prefer
the shorter/more specific row among same-source overlaps; let graded AI evidence override JHU/WHO annuals; refit the
template on onset-week data or a regional pool; flag JHU 26 (2005) and WHO Index 1 (2023 week 35) in the baseline.
