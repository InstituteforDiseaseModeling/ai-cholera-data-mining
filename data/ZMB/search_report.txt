3. THE DISAPPOINTING NUMBERS
- The MOSAIC weekly series uses almost none of this work. py/build_weekly_timeseries.py reads national rows only and ranks
  WHO > JHU > AI. Of its 2,960 weeks, only 557 come from the AI layer, and 366 of those are the 1970-1976 zeros. For every week of
  1977-2012 the series is a JHU annual total spread by a K=2 Fourier template. Hundreds of AI sub-annual national rows (WER
  notifications 1978-2007, MoH monthly series, AFRO weekly tables) and all 1,759 sub-national rows never reach it.
- The builder places 27,021 cases in weeks that the AI layer records as zero-transmission (37 of 53 national zero rows), all from
  read-only JHU annual totals. The largest single error is 10,958 cases in Jul-Dec 2024: weeks with no new cases after
  29 Jun / 11 Jul 2024 (documented absence, rows 367/368/1408) receive up to 1,124 cases/week from the JHU 2024 annual 23,380,
  because WHO's weekly rows for those weeks are blank. The series' 2024 total is 31,190 against about 20,232 actually reported
  (+54%).
- Quote re-verification: 55 of 58 rows strictly correct (94.8%). The national 2023/24 epidemic total (23,382/740), the single
  largest number in the file, carried a citation to a page that never contained it, through the May-2026 run and this cycle's six
  agents, until this audit.
- Eleven windows remain without counts (section 6). For most of them the counts exist only in offline Ministry records.
- 9 of the 58 verifications rest only on collectors' cached copies, because parliament.gov.zm, the WHO Power BI API and
  allafrica.com refused connections from here.
