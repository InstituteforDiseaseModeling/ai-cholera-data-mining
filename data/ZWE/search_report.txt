Bottom line. The AI layer for Zimbabwe went from 70 rows / 36 sources to 3,194 live rows / 458 sources, and all-layer
monthly coverage (Jan 1970 - Oct 2026, 682 months) rose from 76.5% (522 months) to 96.5% (658 months). JHU+WHO alone
cover 30.8% (210 months); 445 months now have data ONLY from the AI layer (309 at the start). The validator passes
(0 ERROR). This is a large, well-documented gain. It is NOT a clean sweep, and the three caveats below matter more for
the MOSAIC model than the headline coverage number.

1. The coverage number overstates the evidence. 177 of the 658 covered months (27%) are covered only because of five
   long AI zero rows (1970-73, 1980-81, 1988-92, 1994-97) and two JHU two-year aggregates (May-Jul 2008). Another 8
   months are covered only by provincial or district zeros while the national picture is unknown: 1983 Jan-Feb and
   Jul-Nov rest on Agent 6's Masvingo-only zeros (rows 3319/3320), and Agent 6 itself classed the national picture for
   those months as non-reporting. 1982 is "covered" only by a deaths-only row (2 registered deaths, row 3244), and no
   case count exists. National rows alone cover 645 months (94.6%).
2. The national weekly series that py/build_weekly_timeseries.py builds from these files is inflated. I ran it in
   memory and nothing was written. The builder keeps the larger of overlapping same-source Fourier shares, and JHU
   outranks AI. Against accepted totals:
     2008-09 epidemic   157,160 cases vs 98,592 (+59%), JHU
     2018-19 outbreak    13,966 vs 10,671 (+31%), JHU
     2023-24 epidemic    38,072 vs 34,549 (+10%), plus 3,304 phantom cases in 2 Jan - 12 Feb 2023 (before the
                         12 Feb onset) from JHU 2023 annual row 51, which overrides AI zero 3323
     calendar 2025          827 vs WER 524 (+58%)
     AI-driven years 1974-2007: +3% to +106%, because WHO annual rows are nested over WER sub-period rows
   The builder also writes 199 "assumed_zero" weeks. Seven of its nine runs overlap documented cholera presence:
   1977 Q1, 1977 Q4 (only through an undated whole-year 1977 row), Dec 1979 - Jan 1980, 1982-83 (104 weeks),
   1987 (51 weeks) and two 2025 runs (5 weeks). JHU baseline row 373 copies
   the 2017 year-to-date count (6 cases, 3 deaths) into week 41 of 2017, overriding the AI documented zero 507.
   These are pipeline defects. Most cannot be fixed from the AI layer (JHU is read-only and outranks AI), so they are
   reported here, not hidden (Appendix G).
3. Six gap periods (706 days) remain open, and the country is 19 days stale. At the start there were 3 gaps
   totalling 4,845 days; the 13-year 1972-1984 hole is now four short windows. The latest positive observation is a
   single suspected case in Chiredzi on 15-16 Sep 2026 (row 55). The WHO dashboard has shown nothing after the week of
   9 March 2026, WHO HQ's cumulative has been frozen since 29 March, and AFRO's bulletin since late January. This is
   during an outbreak that was still active in early August 2026.
